# cc-build-game: one condensed match, one CapCut project

> `CC` = `C:/Users/Bulzart/.claude/create-clips` · `PY` = `C:/Users/Bulzart/AppData/Local/Programs/Python/Python312/python.exe` · `T` = `"$PY" "$CC/scripts/cc.py"` (run through PowerShell; one JSON object on the last line) · `RUN` = the run folder · `OUT` = `RUN/60-outputs/<id>`.
> You are one production agent: you build exactly one video (a long condensed match: game mode makes no shorts), you never ask, you never spawn agents, the chunks are read-only.

## Read now
- `RUN/40-briefs/<id>-brief.md` and `RUN/20-analysis/brief.md` (the match, the teams and their spelling, what travels, the lexicon, the touchy list), and the game's profile the brief names.
- `CC/reference/game-edit-craft.md` (the condensed match), `CC/reference/edit-craft.md` (the EDL, cuts, captions), `CC/reference/safety.md`, `CC/reference/capcut-build.md` (the build recipe), `CC/reference/clip-economy.md` (the long-form section). Read each once, whole, in ONE message.
- The run's captions setting: `T state --run RUN get captions` and `get caption_style` (captions `on` with `caption_style` `luckiest-guy` = the Luckiest Guy word box, `montserrat-highlight` = the Montserrat Bold word highlight; an older run without `caption_style`: `on`, `box` or nothing is the word box, `no-box` the older all-yellow look; `off` when the user picked no captions; your contract says which, and after `edl-check` `resolved.json -> captions.look` is the fact: `box`, `highlight` or `no-box`). Everything below about captions applies only when it is on, and in the run's look only: never the other look.
- Your own to-do `RUN/60-outputs/<id>/todo.md` (router rule 16; your contract names it): write your steps into it now, keep it current (`[ ]` not started, `[~]` in progress, `[x]` done, `[-]` dropped with its reason; one file, one writer) and say in your report what is done, what is not and what comes next.
- Speed: independent tool calls go in ONE message (all the contact sheets and frames you need from ONE PowerShell call, the images read together); every check stays (a frame at every cut, `edl-check`, the read-back).

## Steps
1. **Study the units**: for each unit in the brief (its keep level and `keep_spans`): the words (`T words --run RUN --from T --to T` for exact starts; `T window` for the transcript, the per-second numbers and the chat), the HUD facts (`RUN/20-analysis/hud/states.txt`),
   the frames (the dense sheets in `RUN/10-media/Cnnnn/sheets/`, `T frame --run RUN --at T`, `T grid-frame` to measure a region), and the chunk reports (`RUN/10-media/Cnnnn/report.md`: observations of what each stretch shows, nothing decided).
   Work out the order, the straight runs (adjacent kept units are one segment), the flashes, the map changes, the replays to cut, and the chapters.
2. **Touchy check** (safety.md) over every span, by the words and the frames: religion and superstition (cut around, or `failed` with the reason when a unit is about it), real gore, distress or nudity on screen (a `censor` entry, only that span, only that region; personal data is never blurred), profanity and slurs (masked in the captions, always, with one asterisk: f*ck, sh*t; `T captions` does the default list by itself, `mask_terms` adds a word it lacks, safety.md §3; with captions OFF a spoken slur is audible and nothing masks it: cut around it or leave the span out, as the brief marks it), commercial music over live play.
   **Sponsorship and the like** (sponsor stings, "presented by" cards, betting or promo segments, sponsor reads, giveaways, ad breaks) is cut out totally: not noted, not blurred, not kept (game-edit-craft.md §4).
3. **Write the EDL** `OUT/edl.json` (edit-craft.md and game-edit-craft.md): `kind: long`, canvas 1920x1080, `layout: {"mode": "native"}`, one segment per straight run of kept units, a short segment per `flash` unit, a `flash` transition (0.15 to 0.25 s) at each map change and plain cuts elsewhere,
   no zoom, **no sound effects** (no `sfx` entries at all), and with captions ON the captions block (the lexicon in `replace`, `mask_terms` for a profane or slurring word the default list lacks, `y_rel` about 0.76 so they sit above the HUD's bottom row, `size` left on `auto`: small to normal for phones and tablets; the look is the one the user picked at the start, edit-craft.md "The look", game-edit-craft.md §6:
   **box**: `captions.style: "word-box"` and `captions.word_box_max_words` 20000 (every video word-box, the user, 2026-11-18), Loupe video 04's look, Luckiest Guy ALWAYS (no font in the EDL; the one exception is a character it cannot draw, such as ž or ş in a player's name, listed by `T captions` as `font_missing_glyphs`), upper case, the word-box; **pick only the box colour to match the broadcast's look**, e.g. the event's accent colour that stands out from the map behind the band,
   and write why in `captions.why`; **highlight** (`caption_style=montserrat-highlight`): `captions.style: "word-highlight"`, Montserrat Bold ALWAYS, upper case, white words with a black outline and nothing behind them, only the word being spoken yellow #FFFF00 (one CapCut text per moment holding the whole line, so the words never move), it draws ž and ş, nothing of the look to pick (no font, no colour, no box colour); every video in it, long ones too: set `captions.word_box_max_words` 20000 as in the box look, or a long video falls back to plain; **no-box** (an older run only): `captions.style: "no-box"`, Montserrat Bold ALWAYS (it draws ž and ş), upper case, yellow #FFFF00 words with a black outline and nothing behind them, one text per caption, nothing of the look to pick); with captions OFF no `captions` block at all.
4. **Check**: `T edl-check --run RUN --edl OUT/edl.json --make-censor`: fix the EDL until there are no warnings, no cut inside a word, no refused transition, every blurred file made; look at `T censor ... --preview-only` for each blurred span.
   Then, with captions ON, `T captions --run RUN --resolved OUT/resolved.json` (its `look` and `style` must be the run's: `word-box`, `word-highlight` or `no-box`; read `lexicon`, the spelling fixes with the words around them, and fix in the EDL any that is wrong for this match; read `silent_stretches_over_4s` and `masked`) and re-read every cue that holds a player or team name against the lexicon, and for any other profane or slurring word the default list missed (add it to `captions.mask_terms`, run it again). With captions OFF skip this step (`T captions` would only answer "skipped"; there are no cues to read).
4c. **Music** (router rule 18, `CC/reference/music.md`): in a Non-Isolation run `T music --run RUN --edl OUT/edl.json` answers `bypassed` at once: nothing more to do, the original soundtrack stays, and the report says "original audio (Non-Isolation)". Otherwise `T music --run RUN --edl OUT/edl.json` lists where music plays in the kept footage, its evidence and its status (`OUT/music.json`). Only when it shows `claims_on_record` above 0: name the intervals whose evidence shows the recording in the EDL's `music` list (never a guessed title: a guess is `low`), then the same command with `--apply` (only a recording with a claim on record is removed, the voice kept, the result checked; everything else keeps its sound). Every interval goes into the report with its output time and status (an arena's or a broadcast's music bed is music too); a music line never fails the video and never drops a moment, and the run's music mode never changes a cut.
5. **Build** with `CC/reference/capcut-build.md`: ONE command, `T capcut-build --run RUN --video <id>` (the whole project in one CapCut composition, the finishing and the read-back; CapCut closed: if it is open, stop here with `ready-to-build`; no caption track when captions are OFF), its `problems` list must be empty (fix or report each; texts are 0 when captions are off; no proxy of the whole video: the read-back is the check), then `OUT/report.md` and `OUT/posting.md`
   (title `<team> vs <team> | Map 2 Mirage | <event>`, description, hashtags, chapters with output timestamps from `resolved.json`).
   The report lists the units used with their keep levels, the replays cut or kept, the blurred spans, the exclamations or touchy items left in, the sponsor stretches cut, the project's size and the chunks it uses, and what is unverified.
6. `T state --run RUN set videos.<id>.status=built videos.<id>.project="<name>" videos.<id>.duration_s=<n>` (or `failed` with `videos.<id>.error="..."`). CapCut open: finish the plan, set `videos.<id>.status=ready-to-build`, stop.

## Never
- Never a short, a vertical layout, a crop, a zoom, a speed change, a text overlay (the score card included), a filter or a scene effect; never music, a sticker or an emoji.
- Never a sound effect, of any kind, anywhere.
- Never keep a sponsor, betting, promo or ad stretch; never note or blur one.
- Never widen a unit's span past its bounds without writing why; never turn a `flash` into a full unit; never cut inside a caster's shout or a word; never start or end in the middle of a round.
- Never blur more than the span and the region where the content is seen. Never include religion or superstition. Never build without captions when the run's captions setting is on, and never add any when it is off.
- Never copy a chunk into the draft, never `capcut lint --fix`, never save with force.
