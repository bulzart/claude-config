# cc-plan: the portfolio

> `CC`, `PY`, `T`, `RUN` as in cc-start.

## Read now
- `CC/reference/portfolio.md` (merge, long-form richness, shorts selection, the briefs). Read it whole.
- `CC/reference/clip-economy.md` (what travels, the shape of a short and of a long video). Read it whole.
- `CC/reference/context-keeping.md` (short: the dull parts of a long video and the context a bit of one may carry).
- `RUN/20-analysis/brief.md` and `clip-criteria.md`, and every `RUN/20-analysis/blocks/block-NN.json`.

## Steps
1. **Merge** (portfolio.md §1): write `stream-summary.md`, `moments.json` (each moment's `heat` kept as the miner wrote it; the second look's moments from `RUN/20-analysis/second-look.json` too, with their `SL-` ids, `found_by` and `why_missed`), `richness.json` (a chunk's hot share in `RUN/20-analysis/heat.md` is a small clue when you smooth a score: `CC/reference/chat-heat.md`), `acts.json` (the mining agents' `acts`, merged in rec time: an act that is open at the end of one block is joined to its continuation in the next), `lexicon.json` (merged with the profile's; only when captions are on: `T state --run RUN get captions` is not `off`), `sensitive.json`, `avoid.json`, and `ad-batches.json` (the mining agents' `ad_batches`, merged by rec time). Where a block is missing or thin, read its transcripts yourself (`T window`).
   **The ad batches are left out here** (the user, 2026-11-14: the recorder skips nothing, ad or not ad; the agents exclude the ad batches when they analyse): every batch with verdict `ad` goes into `avoid.json` as `kind: "ad"`, so every brief carries it as a span to stay away from and no run, span or pickup includes it. A chunk the recorder marked (`T blocks` lists `ad_batches`; `(ad-marked)` in stream.md) that no mining agent judged is judged by you now from its report and a sheet.
1b. **A check of what will be used** (you, the model the user picked): the block files are the mining agents' reading, each with its own `standalone` and `shareability` scores. Before a moment becomes a short or a span of a long video,
   read its words yourself (`T window --run RUN --from T --to T`) and look at a dense sheet or a frame of its payoff (`RUN/10-media/Cnnnn/sheets/`, `T frame`) or at the chunk's report (`RUN/10-media/Cnnnn/report.md`): fix the bounds, the hook and the scores where the card is wrong and drop what does not hold. A hook quoted from a `[sub]` line is the game's line, not the streamer's: keep it only when the game's line is the point, else open on the streamer's reaction. A moment's `heat` is read the same way (`chat-heat.md`): a nudge that rests on a burst the words and the frames do not explain is taken back.
   The best three shorts, the first and last span of every long video, every moment with `confidence` low or medium, and every moment the second look recovered (`found_by: "second-look"`: one reader found it, a second one checks it) always get it. A recovered moment is held to the same bar as any other: it is chosen only when it beats what it would replace, and more moments never mean more videos (the shorts level and the long videos' content decide the counts).
   Make all the sheets and frames you need in ONE call (several `T sheet` / `T frame` lines in one PowerShell command) and read the images together in one message.
2. **Decide the long-form videos** (portfolio.md §2): the acts, the runs and pickups from the richness line, where the borders between the videos fall and how many videos the night earns. **How much of the stream becomes videos is your judgement and never a target**: from an 8-hour stream three hours of videos if the analysis says so, or 32 minutes in all, or none; nothing is included to fill a video or to reach a number of minutes. **Each video is one whole watch, usually about 25 minutes to an hour when the content is there, set by the content and not by the clock** (a 7-hour stream might give 36, 43 and 27 minutes): the borders fall between acts, never an hour filled and the rest left for the next video, never a story left half told in one video and finished in the next, and **every border is checked by reading the words around it** (the user, 2026-11-14). **The dull parts** are left out; where something that is kept leans on a bit of one, you may keep that bit as a context span (`context-keeping.md` says where the leads are and how to find the place in the whole recording, across blocks too; checked in the words and a frame; the data decides). **What a long video is for, and its form** (the user, 2026-11-20; portfolio.md §2): read the brief's long-video line first (how this streamer's long-form viewers watch; a line tagged [I] is a guess and is read as one); tell the three kinds of footage apart (worth watching, what a kept part needs, what gives the viewer nothing: `clip-economy.md`, judged for this streamer and this audience and never because a stretch is quiet); give each video the form the content earns (a story episode, a condensed run, a best-of, or another; no form is the default); read the set as a viewer would (no beat repeated without something new); and write each long brief's editorial notes (portfolio.md §4). A plan that keeps nearly the whole stream says why this audience would watch it nearly whole. **The user's pick at the start** (`T state --run RUN get only`): `only=shorts` makes no long-form video; `long` and `both` (and a run with none, made before the question existed) plan them as far as the stream earns any (it may earn none).
3. **Decide the shorts** (portfolio.md §3): which moments, how many (**the user's level, `T state --run RUN get shorts_level`: `concentrated` = only the one, two or three best, the most highlighted pieces with the most potential; `normal` = about 5 to 10**; a range the user chose and never a quota, so fewer when fewer are worth making and none when none is; a run with no level, made before the question existed: as many as are good; up to 3 minutes each, the length follows the moment), what the hook is, what is cut together and what is cut twice. Among moments of about the same worth the one the audience reacted to harder (its `heat`) goes first, and never ahead of a clearly better one (portfolio.md §3). **`only=long` (the user chose long videos only at the start): none, no short is planned, briefed or built**; `shorts` and `both` (and a run with none) plan them as far as the stream earns any.
4. **Write the briefs** (portfolio.md §4): `RUN/40-briefs/<id>-brief.md` for every video (`S01`, `S02`, ... and `L01`, `L02`, ...), and `RUN/20-analysis/portfolio.md` (the table, the counts, the building order: best first). Each brief carries its spans (a context span marked as one, with what it gives and what needs it), the touchy spans with what to blur (gore, distress, nudity only), the avoid spans, the lexicon, the people and a title and hashtags idea; a long video's brief also says how it opens and how it ends and names the video before and after it (where each stops and starts). Each source span also carries one line of its chat heat (`T heat --run RUN --from T --to T`: where chat peaked and what it said, or "no burst"), a clue for the builder's cuts and payoff.
   Every span in a brief is re-checked against `avoid.json` (religion and superstition never in; an incidental one is edited around or the moment is dropped) and `sensitive.json` (kept, blurred where seen).
   Each brief states the run's captions setting (`T state --run RUN get captions`). With captions OFF a slur that is spoken stays audible (nothing masks it and no audio is edited): the brief marks every span where one is heard, and the plan cuts around it or drops the span (`CC/reference/safety.md`).
5. Record the videos with dotted keys (no JSON quoting): `T state --run RUN set videos.S01.kind=short "videos.S01.title=working title" videos.S01.status=planned` for each, and `stage=planned next_skill=cc-produce`.
   (`T state` values are read as JSON when they parse, as a file when written `key=@file.json`, else as text.)
   Then add one build item per planned video to `RUN/todo.md` (rule 16: its id, its brief and its output folder by full path), tick the plan and set `Next:` to the production.
6. Give the user nothing to answer: the plan is theirs to read later in `portfolio.md`. One line in the chat says how many shorts and long videos are planned (with the shorts level the user picked; when the shorts are fewer than the level's range, the line says so and why in a few words).

## Never
- Never plan a video to a quota or to hit a length (an hour, or 25 minutes), and never include a stretch only to fill a video or to reach a number of minutes: how much of the stream is worth turning into videos is yours to judge, and the answer can be hours, half an hour or none. Never divide the stream by the clock, and never leave a story half in one long video and the rest of it in the next. Never plan padding. Never plan a moment that is about religion or superstition.
- Never write the cut list: the production agent decides exact cuts, zooms, sounds and captions from the brief.

Invoke Skill `cc-produce`.
