## Steps
1. **The mode, the captions (on or off and, when on, the caption style) and (streamer mode) the videos choice, and with shorts their level, come first, and the user picks them** (nothing here guesses the mode, the captions or the videos). `$ARGUMENTS` may carry `mode=streamer` or `mode=game`, `captions=on` or `captions=off`, `caption_style=luckiest-guy` or `caption_style=montserrat-highlight`, `only=long`, `only=both` or `only=shorts`, and `shorts=concentrated` or `shorts=normal`: what is given is not asked (`captions=on` without `caption_style` asks only the style). Older forms still work: `captions=box` is captions on in the Luckiest Guy style; `captions=no-box` is the older all-yellow Montserrat look (one text per caption) of runs started before the style question: kept so such a command line still works, never offered.
   A run whose `state.json` already holds a `mode` (a resume) keeps it, and keeps its `captions` and `caption_style` and asks neither again: a run made before the captions option has no `captions` (captions on, Luckiest Guy); one made before the style question has `captions` `on` or `box` (Luckiest Guy) or `no-box` (the older yellow look) and no `caption_style`, and keeps that look: nothing is added to its state. It also keeps its `only` (a run made before the question existed has none: that means `both`) and its `shorts_level` (a run made before the question existed has none: the planner then judges the number of shorts as before).
   Otherwise, before anything else, ask with AskUserQuestion, **all the missing questions in ONE call** (one dialog, headers `Mode`, `Captions` and `Videos`; `Videos` only when the mode is streamer or is asked in the same call, and never in game mode):
   - `Mode`: "Which kind of clipping is this run?" with two options:
     - **Streamer clipping**: "A person is the show (jokes, hype, arguments, chat, lingo). Research of the streamer, their audience and the topic; short-form videos (up to 3 minutes) and long-form videos, with zooms and sound effects (and captions if you pick them)."
     - **Game clipping**: "A match is the show (BLAST Premier CS2, a Dota 2 game, Valorant, League). Research of the game, the event and the teams; every round or fight decided (full, trimmed, a flash or skipped); long condensed-match videos only, one video and one CapCut project per game (a recording with several different matches gives each its own), no shorts, no sound effects; sponsor stretches are cut."
   - `Captions`: "Enable captions?" with two options, **no default and no "Recommended" mark: the user picks** (the user, 2026-11-20, "Add Montserrat Caption Option Without Replacing Luckiest Guy": captions on or off first, then, when on, the style; the Luckiest Guy word box stays exactly as it is, the Montserrat Bold word highlight is a second option beside it):
     - **On**: "Every second of speech is captioned (names fixed from the research), in a small to normal size for phones, in the caption style you pick next."
     - **Off**: "The same cuts, zooms and blurs, nothing typed on the picture: no caption track is made. A slur spoken aloud stays audible, so the plan cuts around such lines."
   - `Videos` (streamer mode only; the answer is ignored when the user picks Game clipping, which makes long videos only): "Which videos should this run make?" with two options, **no default and no "Recommended" mark: the user picks**:
     - **Long videos only**: "Long-form videos, each one a whole watch (usually about 25 minutes to an hour) split where the stream's own stories end so nothing is left half told: straight runs of the stream and pickups. The agents decide how much of the stream is worth it: hours of videos from a great night, half an hour from a quiet one, none from a dull one. No shorts."
     - **Long videos + shorts**: "The long-form videos (whole watches of about 25 minutes to an hour) and short-form ones too (Shorts, TikTok, Reels: always 9:16, up to 3 minutes each; you pick how many next), planned together and built at the same time."
   **The follow-ups** (they depend on the first answers, so they cannot be in the same call): as soon as the first dialog is answered, ask what applies of the two below in ONE more AskUserQuestion call (headers `Style` and `Shorts`); nothing is asked when neither applies.
   **The caption style** (the user, 2026-11-20): only when captions are on (the `On` answer or `captions=on`) and no `caption_style` was given or stored, header `Style`: "Which caption style?" with two options, **no default and no "Recommended" mark: the user picks**:
     - **Luckiest Guy — Word Box (Existing)**: "The current look, kept as it is (Loupe video 04's): Luckiest Guy, upper case, white words with a black outline, the spoken word on a rounded colour box that moves word to word, the box colour matched to each video; the box a tiny bit smaller since 2026-11-20."
     - **Montserrat Bold — Word Highlight (New)**: "Montserrat Bold, upper case, white words with a black outline and nothing behind them; only the word being spoken turns yellow (#FFFF00), in time with the speech, the words before and after it white; the line stays still, no other animation."
   Never asked when captions are off.
   **The shorts level** (the user, 2026-11-14): when the run will make shorts (streamer mode, `only` is `both` or `shorts`) and `shorts=` was not given, header `Shorts`: "How many shorts should this run make?" with two options, **no default and no "Recommended" mark: the user picks**:
     - **Concentrated**: "Only the one, two or three best shorts: the most highlighted pieces of the content, the ones with the most potential, each given the most care. Fewer if fewer are worth it."
     - **Normal**: "About 5 to 10 shorts across the stream's good moments. Fewer if the stream does not have that many worth making: nothing is made just to reach a number."
   The level is a range the user chose, never a quota (`cc-plan` makes fewer when fewer are worth it). Not asked when no shorts are made (`only=long`, game mode).
   The answers are the mode, the captions setting (and with captions on the style), the videos choice and (with shorts) the shorts level; "Other" text that names one of the options is taken as that one (`Luckiest Guy` or `box` is the word box, `Montserrat` or `highlight` the word highlight, `shorts only` is taken as `only=shorts`), anything else: ask once more. If the questions cannot be asked (a non-interactive run), the mode must come as `mode=streamer` or `mode=game` (stop and say so otherwise), the captions must come as `captions=off`, or `captions=on` with `caption_style=luckiest-guy` or `caption_style=montserrat-highlight` (or the older `captions=box` / `captions=no-box`; stop and say so otherwise: the style is the user's pick), in streamer mode `only` must come as an argument (stop and say so otherwise), and when shorts are made `shorts=concentrated` or `shorts=normal` must come as an argument too (stop and say so otherwise).
   `T state --run RUN set mode=streamer captions=on caption_style=luckiest-guy only=both shorts_level=normal` (or `game`, `caption_style=montserrat-highlight`, or `captions=off` with no `caption_style`, `long`, `concentrated`; `On` is stored as `on`, `Off` as `off`, **Luckiest Guy — Word Box** as `luckiest-guy`, **Montserrat Bold — Word Highlight** as `montserrat-highlight`: `T state` refuses any other style and changes nothing; the older argument `captions=box` is stored as `captions=on caption_style=luckiest-guy`, `captions=no-box` as it is; game mode records `only=long` without asking, and `shorts_level` is left out when no shorts are made) once the run is known (step 3), and `game="..."` when the user gave it.
2. **Arguments** (`$ARGUMENTS`): the run folder (a path, or a session name under `runs_root`), then optional `only=long|both|shorts` (streamer mode: the answer to the `Videos` question, so it is not asked when given; game mode makes long videos only and `only=shorts` is refused in one line), `shorts=concentrated|normal` (streamer mode: the answer to the `Shorts` question, so it is not asked when given; ignored when no shorts are made), `notes="..."` (what the user knows: the guest, the topic, what they want), `streamer="Name"`, `game="Counter-Strike 2"`, and, optional and never asked, the user's own pick of the caption box colour: `captions_color="#E02B3A"` (the Luckiest Guy word box only: the Montserrat word highlight has nothing behind the text and its colours are fixed, so it is not used; say so in one line).
   The caption font is not an option: it is the look's, always: Luckiest Guy for the Luckiest Guy word box (the user, 2026-11-13: "always use the same font family as they used in that video"), Montserrat Bold for the Montserrat word highlight (white, the spoken word yellow) and for the older no-box look (the user, 2026-11-20); edit-craft.md "The look". Without `captions_color` the production agents pick the box colour per video to match its appearance; with it every word-box video of the run uses the user's colour wherever its EDL leaves it open.
   No run given: `T status` lists the runs; with exactly one run that has chunks, take it, otherwise report the list and stop (the only other time to ask).
3. **The run**: `T status --run <arg>` (stages per chunk, the report column, free space, totals). Take what is there: a chunk with stage `failed` or `pending` is listed and skipped; `T chunks --run RUN` is the table.
   The recording may still be growing (a watcher running): `T state --run RUN get recording` and `get stream_ended` say whether the recorder has ended it (the stream ended, it was stopped, a limit was reached: `reason`); when it has not ended, the chunk table at this moment is the run for this pass.
   `T status` also prints `ready` (the recording ended, every chunk processed, every report settled: the watcher then showed the user a Windows notification) and, when it is false, `why_not`: say in one line what is still running; it is information, not a stop, the rule above stands.
   Record the chunks used (`T state --run RUN set snapshot_chunks=<n>`) and use only those chunks. No processed chunk at all: stop and say what to run (`T watch`).
4. **Facts to hold**: channel, date, number of chunks and total length, the title and category changes (`00-inventory/stream-meta.json`), the source quality (`probe.json` of a chunk), gaps, the spoken language (the `Spoken language` line of `stream.md`, `language` in `T status`: `CC/reference/language.md`), whether the chat was recorded (the `chat total/max` column of `stream.md`; `T heat` in step 6 says `has_chat`), the ad-marked chunks (`T status` prints `ad_marked`; `T blocks` lists the batches: the recorder skips nothing, ad or not ad, and Twitch's marker is a fact the mining agents judge, see `CC/reference/moment-mining.md`), free space on the drive.
   Read `RUN/00-inventory/stream.md` once (the table of numbers per chunk).
5. **Folders**: `RUN/20-analysis/blocks`, `RUN/40-briefs`, `RUN/60-outputs`, `RUN/deliverables` exist (`New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force`); game mode also `RUN/20-analysis/hud`.
   **Write `RUN/todo.md`** (router rule 16, the user, 2026-11-13: tasks and to-dos so that what is done and what comes next is never only in a conversation): a first line `# Run to-do: <run id> (kept by the main session; one writer)`, a `Next:` line, an `Updated:` line, the marks (`[ ]` not started, `[~]` in progress, `[x]` done, `[-]` dropped with its reason), then every phase still ahead as checklist items (the start answers, the profile and the research note, each mining block, the plan, each video's build, the wrap-up); make the session's tasks from them when it has task tools (TaskCreate, TaskUpdate); each later phase adds its own items when it starts and ticks the finished one. A resumed run already has it: read it first.
6. **Start everything that does not wait for anything else, together, in ONE message** (speed: nothing below needs another's result):
   - **The one research agent**, in the background (Agent tool, general-purpose, no `model` argument, web only), briefed from `CC/reference/research-agent.md`: its contract for this mode, the run, the channel, the date, the spoken language, the stream's title and category from `stream-meta.json`, the user's `notes`, `streamer` or `game`, its output `RUN/20-analysis/research.md`, its budget (`config.json -> agents.research_calls` tool calls, a short note, not a deep research). It is the only research agent of the command (`config.json -> agents.research_max`). `T state --run RUN set research.status=running`.
     The recon, the HUD map and the scan of the next phases run while it works; `cc-profile` / `cc-profile-game` collect its note.
   - **The chunk reports** (made while the chunks were downloaded: a headless Haiku session per chunk reads its sheets, audio numbers, transcript and chat and writes `RUN/10-media/Cnnnn/report.md`; observations only, `CC/reference/reporter.md`): the `reports` counts of `T status` say how many are `ok`, `missing`, `stale`, `failed`, `running`.
     Complete what is not there: `T report --run RUN --parallel 4` (every chunk without a current report; the sessions run side by side, about a minute each; it prints the count, the cost and anything that failed). A chunk whose report cannot be made (no claude CLI, a failure twice) is listed: the analysis reads its raw data instead.
   - **Streamer mode:** `T sfx-scan --run RUN` catalogues the sound-effects folder (`30-assets/sfx.md`; it fails plainly when the folder is missing); game mode has no sound effects and skips it.
   - **The chat heat** (the user, 2026-11-15): `T heat --run RUN` counts the chat's messages in 4-second bins of rec time against the stream's own normal and lists the bursts (`RUN/20-analysis/heat.md` and `heat.json`; local, a few seconds; `CC/reference/chat-heat.md`). Facts only. A run with no chat recorded answers `has_chat: false`: the heat is skipped everywhere and that is not a stop. Rerun it if more chunks have arrived since.
   - `T doctor` must show ffmpeg and ffprobe working; game mode also needs the screen reader (it shows whether `rapidocr_onnxruntime` loads; when it does not, say so: the analysis then works from the casters' words, the reports and the sheets).
   CapCut running is noted (the build needs it closed), not a blocker yet.
7. **Size the work**: `T config get agents` shows `scan_chunks_streamer` (10) and `scan_chunks_game` (8): the analysis makes one block per that many chunks (the agents read the reports, so a block is large), at least `scan_min_blocks` (2) blocks so that even a short recording is read by agents side by side, at most `scan_max` (6) agents at a time. A 4-hour stream is about 8 blocks; a short stream is two.
   The user's `notes`, `streamer`, `only` and `captions_color` go into `state.json` (`T state --run RUN set notes="..." only=long captions_color="#E02B3A"`). `shorts_level` is the answer to the `Shorts` question (`concentrated` or `normal`; `cc-plan` reads it). `only` is the answer to the `Videos` question: `long` plans and builds no short, `both` plans both kinds, `shorts` (an argument only) no long video; `cc-plan` reads it.
8. `T state --run RUN set stage=started next_skill=cc-profile` (streamer) or `next_skill=cc-profile-game` (game), then tick the intake items in `RUN/todo.md` and set its `Next:` line (rule 16).

## Never
- Never move, rename, delete or rewrite a chunk, a sheet, a transcript or a report. The run's media is read-only for the whole command.
- Never guess the mode and never change it later: the user picked it; the same for the captions and their style, the videos choice (long only, or long plus shorts) and the shorts level. Never ask anything but the mode, the captions choice (on or off, and the style when on), the videos choice (streamer mode), the shorts level (only when shorts are made) and, when it is missing, the run.
- Never start a second research agent, never a research wave.

## Hand-off
Invoke Skill `cc-profile` (streamer mode) or `cc-profile-game` (game mode).
