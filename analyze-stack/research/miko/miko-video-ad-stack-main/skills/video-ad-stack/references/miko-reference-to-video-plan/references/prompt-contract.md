# Reference-to-product prompt contract

Read the sourced brand brief and completed reference breakdown. In the four-stage stack, require ffmpeg/frame inspection and Higgsfield analysis on matching coverage. Save an adaptation table: source beat/time, narrative function, retained mechanism, changed product/message and supporting evidence. Preserve the measured duration as the target. Keep source dialogue distinct from the rewritten brand script.

Write `production/master-prompt.md` with these sections:

1. Duration, aspect ratio and observed style.
2. Product-image attachment map: what each image governs, including bottle shape, cap, label, materials and color. Use the selected provider's actual reference syntax at execution; never invent universal image tags.
3. Scene, subject, wardrobe, spatial object positions, lighting and camera behavior.
4. Ordered timed action beats, with precise framing, camera motion, hands/product contact, reveal timing and expressions. Avoid contradictory simultaneous actions.
5. Audio: exact new dialogue, voice descriptions, delivery, ambience, music and effects. Preserve intentionally silent formats rather than forcing speech.
6. Continuity: product, recurring character, wardrobe, environment and voice. Repeat needed details in each independently generated clip; “same as before” is insufficient alone.
7. Exclusions: source-brand overlays, accidental captions and unrelated objects. Preserve intended physical product-label text; requested final captions belong in editing.

Use text direction PLUS the user's product-image references. A product image governs identity; it is not automatically the first frame. Choose a reference-capable endpoint, not a pure text-only mode that cannot accept images. Do not upload competitor footage to generation by default.

If supported duration permits one generation, provide one prompt and its attachments. Otherwise give numbered clip prompts, planned durations summing to the target, continuity/boundary actions and assembly order. Flag duration limitations before spend; measure actual final duration. Do not speed speech to conceal timing errors. A generated clip may serve as a continuity reference when supported and needed.

Match the selected reference's format. Do not force a fixed street-interview structure onto unrelated references.
