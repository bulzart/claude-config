# Reference to video plan

Read the supplied brand brief and reference breakdowns. Preserve the chosen product, format and duration. Do not force cinematic references into talking-head presets. Flag missing evidence without inventing product facts.

Write `production/video-plan.md` with each concept's audience hypothesis, adapted reference mechanism and original treatment. Include exact script and on-screen copy, then ordered scene IDs, estimated durations, action, camera, lighting, sound and product visibility. Separate factual claims from creative language; never present a fictional actor as a verified customer.

Specify official product references and continuity requirements for bottle/label, creator, wardrobe, environment and voice. Map shots to production operations: starting frame, reference-driven video, native speech, editing or assembly. Verify current Higgsfield model constraints before giving exact parameters. Mark unsupported operations.

Describe clip order, trims, transitions and captions if requested. Speech timing estimates must be checked against actual audio. Offer a small number of distinct concepts when volume is unspecified. Choose perfume treatments from reference evidence rather than a compulsory format list.

Return a generation-ready plan and unresolved decisions. This skill does not submit paid jobs.
