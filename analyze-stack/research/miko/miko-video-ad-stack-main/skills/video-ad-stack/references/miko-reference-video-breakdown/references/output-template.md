# Breakdown output contract

Write one document per reference, with linked segment evidence when split.

## Source and coverage

File/link, selected segment, source offset, inspection methods completed, missing layers and uncertainty.

## Specs

Duration, dimensions, FPS, audio presence, confirmed cut timestamps/count, visual-change candidate count with threshold, distinct filming setups and words/minute with denominator. Separate exact metadata from estimates.

## Transcript or dialogue summary

Time range, speaker label and words; retain slang and delivery where transcription is appropriate. Mark uncertain words/speakers. Keep source dialogue distinct from any later adapted script.

## Shot-by-shot

| Source time | Framing | Camera move | On screen / props | Action and delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|

One row per confirmed shot; describe important within-shot beats. Record gestures, expressions, product contact and timing rather than generic mood labels.

## People and setups

Appearance, hair, makeup, wardrobe, jewelry, nails and gestures per setup. Note changes across the hook and main take.

## Setting and image

Objects with positions, surfaces, lighting direction/quality, grade and visible camera characteristics. Distinguish inferred equipment from observable image traits.

## Text and overlays

Timestamped captions, titles, watermarks, handles, emojis and UI styling. Separate added overlays to exclude from physical text to preserve. Identify legibility gaps.

## Audio

Music character/continuity/ducking, effects and timestamps, mic quality, room acoustics and other voices. Do not invent a soundtrack name.

## Retention mechanisms

Specific observed hooks, open loops, interrupts, show-while-telling and payoffs; explain hypothesized function without claiming measured performance.

## Measurements and disagreements

Metric, method, value, confidence/limitation. List analyzer/transcript/frame conflicts and resolutions.

## Handoff

Transferable structure, reference-specific elements to replace, exact product/identity references needed, difficult shots and continuity requirements. Optional faithful recreation prompt only when requested; brand adaptation belongs in the new ad plan.
