The user is a software engineer with web experience, living in Kosovo and Muslim. They want to start e-commerce without selling goods they do not own: buy a bulk batch from a Chinese supplier, hold it in a warehouse, then sell and have orders shipped automatically.

They will not bring the goods to Kosovo and plan to sell worldwide. They plan to build the store with AI agents and want every claim in guides based on official or verified sources, written as a document they can act on alone.

**Why:** Shariah rule on not selling what one does not own, plus Kosovo constraints (Stripe, Shopify Payments, Wise personal, PayPal and Payoneer do not serve Kosovo residents), so a foreign company is needed.

**How to apply:** Give sourced, Kosovo-aware advice; label estimates and unverified items; keep Shariah steps (possession before sale, no interest-based credit) in any plan; defer final rulings to a scholar. See [[ecommerce-playbook-doc]].
