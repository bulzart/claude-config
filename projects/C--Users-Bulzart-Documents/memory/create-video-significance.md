The user watched the finished spy film (v3) several times and still needed an outside explanation of why German troops
waiting near Calais mattered ("is it only that he left the soldiers wait? whats the big plot?"). Brief #5: "It is not enough
for viewers to understand what happened. They should also understand why the important developments matter within the
experience they came to watch." The user's English is not their first language: a reason carried only by an idiom or a
verdict afterwards ("might well have tipped the balance") did not reach them.

**Why:** fresh LLM readers know the history and supply the missing reason themselves; five final reviews rated v3 8/10
and none caught it. The first version of the readers' question (brief 2) also passed it: the reader explained the
significance as "the trick fooled Germany", which is what happened one level up, not what it changed.

**How to apply:**
- Key moments in the beat map carry `significance` (matters_because, reward, depends_on or self_contained; contested
  claims name a `hedge` line); plan-check refuses one without it. Significance is the video's own reward (a laugh, an
  answer, awe), never manufactured stakes.
- Script and final readers (brief 3) give, per development: what happened, why it matters from the video only,
  would_be_different, learned_when (before / at the moment / only afterwards / never), paid off; review-record turns gaps
  into S findings (major: unclear, own knowledge, never, only afterwards on a key moment) and K findings.
- Fix the cause, not with more narration by default: setup before the moment, a picture that shows the relation, another
  order. Judge comprehension for a first-time, possibly non-native viewer.
- Locks don't dismiss evidence-supported improvements, but the length lock (604.5-635.5 s here) still holds unless the
  user re-approves: find the words elsewhere.
