## 3. Asset registry — `30-assets/registry.md` and `state.json → assets`

One row per asset:

| id | type | role | files (local) | canonical? | variant_of | views | real size / materials / notes | affordances (parts · openings · sequence · use + contact · contents/count · states · mark · impossible) | uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Canonical templates** are authoritative for size, proportions, shape, curves, silhouette,
  construction and packaging structure.
- **Variants** may change surface appearance only. They inherit the canonical geometry unless the user
  says otherwise.
- **Characters:** when several approved characters exist and no assignment is given, rotate them
  across videos to balance usage and fit each direction. Record `uses`, and do not reuse the same
  character every time. A character's anchor image (static §5) is made from the asset's photos and is the file every still of that
  character is made from.

Scope: `project`. The assets were dropped once at the start, and every video picks from them through the plan without asking again.
Only this registry feeds an image.
