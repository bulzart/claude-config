# League of Legends match broadcasts

Written from model memory [M]: the broadcast wins over this page (objectives and the names of monsters change from season to season). Where the page and the picture disagree, trust the picture and note it.

## The match
- Two teams of five (Blue and Red) on one map, one game at a time; a series is BO1, BO3 or BO5 with a champion draft (bans and picks) before each game. No rounds: a game runs about 25 to 45 minutes and ends when a team's **Nexus** falls.
- Phases: laning, the mid game (neutral objectives and tower pushes), the late game (one team fight can end it). Gold and the objective count decide who leads.
- Objectives: towers and inhibitors, the dragons (and the Elder Dragon late), **Baron Nashor** (a strong buff for a push), other neutral monsters that vary by season, the Nexus.

## What the broadcast shows
The game clock, each team's kills, gold, towers and dragons at the top; the champions' panels at the sides; the kill feed; instant replays marked; the draft screen before each game; a desk between games. Measure the places on a real frame (`T grid-frame`).

## What a highlight is
A **pentakill** ("penta"), a **team wipe** ("ace"), a **Baron steal** or a **dragon steal**, a **Nexus snipe** or a **backdoor**, an outplay (a flash dodge, a one-hit survival), a **shutdown** (the killing of a snowballing enemy), a comeback from a deficit,
a perfect engage or a **disengage** that saves a team, a throw (a won game given away), **first blood**, a caster who loses it, a crowd that explodes.
Lexicon: gank, dive, kite, peel, engage, disengage, front-to-back, flash, TP, ace, first blood, tempo, scaling, int, throw, comeback, steal, penta.

## The unit: the fight
As in Dota 2 (`dota2.md`): a **fight** (a cluster of kills and abilities within about 20 seconds), an **objective event**, a **phase** or a **swing**; read the units from the kill counts changing in bursts, the objective icons, the casters and the frames.
Quiet but not noise: first blood, a neutral-objective setup that decides a game, the fight before a comeback or a throw. Usually noise: laning and farming, long positioning. Never skip: the game's turning fight, the last fight and the Nexus, a steal, a penta.
Traps: replays; the clock freezing in a pause; the desk cutting away from a fight that is still going; fog of war (you only see what the observer shows).
