### 3.7 References (image / video / audio): syntax, roles, what is inherited and what must be excluded

No image, style-video or audio inputs [OFFICIAL]. The single video is the edit source. Everything is inherited except what the prompt changes. Overlays, usernames and watermarks are therefore inherited unless named (§3.11d).
- **Original reference (Recreate and Edit Mode, every placed product; `reference/generation-system.md` §4b.5):** the single edit source is the original's silent, unmarked footage (≤15 s, §2.2) and no input rides beside it; the spot and size ride in words only (the anchor sentence with the size reckoning); the plan takes an anchor from the passing list or writes the size risk in the brief.
