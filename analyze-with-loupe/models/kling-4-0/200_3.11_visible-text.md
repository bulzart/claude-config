### 3.11 Visible text

Policy: **Edit Mode** removes all source text unless the user keeps specific text; **Analyze Mode** may add intentional new text, by default in deterministic post (CapCut / `awl.py captions`); reference wording, usernames and watermarks are never copied; no emoji in any text, ever (command item 6).

- **(a) Reliability.** Kling claims "Accurate Text Generation: Supports clear and stable generation of multilingual text, emojis, and logos. Even with dynamic camera movement and scene transitions, text remains clear and stable, keeping key information accurate and readable." and shows burned-in subtitles timed from the prompt [OFFICIAL S1]; Higgsfield repeats it as on-screen text in nine languages plus emoji and logos [HF S4]. No independent spelling test was found [INFERRED gap]. Verdict: promising, unverified; hooks and captions still go to post.
- **(b) Clean plate (default in Analyze Mode).** The model "may add" subtitles by default [OFFICIAL S1], so every prompt carries the exclusion, in Kling's own wording: "No text overlays, subtitles, or music." / "no subtitles, no watermarks, no UI" / "Do not add any characters, change the store structure or camera movement, or introduce subtitles, logos, watermarks, or extra text." / "No real brands, organization names, logos, or watermarks may appear." [OFFICIAL S1] (Kling's guide also writes that background text and map labels "must be blurred and unreadable"; this library never writes a blur into a prompt, item 37: background text is described as absent or plain). Closing line used here: "No subtitles, captions, on-screen text, watermarks, logos or UI overlays appear." [INFERRED, built from the official items]. Because the model writes text well, a described sign, screen or package will likely be lettered: describe such surfaces as plain and unmarked, and never quote a non-dialogue string [INFERRED]. With reference media add "do not reproduce any text, captions, usernames or watermarks from @Video1" [INFERRED from the official "Never inherit" pattern]. Never cut these lines to fit a length limit; cut descriptive wording instead [INFERRED].
- **(c) Intentional in-frame text** (scene text only: packaging, a sign, a screen). Kling's pattern: the exact words in backticks or quotes, the surface, the placement and the stability terms — "Fix a clear, stable, readable sign with the exact uppercase text `KLING AI OPEN 24 hours` in the middle of the convenience store's right-side glass window. The text must remain correctly spelled, undistorted, and free of flickering throughout the video. Keep the sign fixed to the inside of the glass window without obstructing any characters." [OFFICIAL S1]. An approved product label comes from the reference image and is held by a QUALITY line: "The white Kling wordmark remains correctly spelled, stable, and attached to the same chest position." [OFFICIAL S1]. Keep it short; QC every frame with `awl.py qc --mode analyze --allowed-text "<text>"` [INFERRED].
- **(d) Edit operations: removing source text (Edit Mode).** Text removal is not documented [gap]; the official removal pattern is "Edit @Video 1 to remove the foreground person. Keep everything else unchanged, including the background, other subjects, camera movement, lighting, composition, and video duration. Seamlessly reconstruct the areas previously obscured by the person, ensuring natural, consistent details with no visible artifacts." [OFFICIAL S1]. Applied to text, as its own first pass (Higgsfield advises changing one element at a time [HF S4]): "Edit @Video1 to remove every caption, subtitle, username, watermark, sticker, timestamp and overlay logo. Keep everything else unchanged, including the people, their movements, camera movement, lighting, composition, audio and video duration. Seamlessly reconstruct the areas previously covered by the text with natural, consistent details and no visible artifacts. No subtitles or on-screen text appear." [INFERRED]. Keep a named source text only on the user's request ("keep only the lettering on the mug"). No local tool removes an overlay or a caption (item 37); the prompt lines do it. Verify with `awl.py qc --mode edit`.
- **(e) Watermarks.** Kling's free plans watermark outputs [SECONDARY S6]; no Higgsfield watermark is documented [gap]. `awl.py qc` OCR catches invented corner marks.
