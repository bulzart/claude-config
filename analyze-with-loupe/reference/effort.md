# Effort level (contract item 33): how densely a run samples and inspects, and what never changes

The user picks the run's effort level at the start, in both modes (`awl-start` step 5d). It sets how densely the references are sampled
and how densely QC renders its checkpoints, the two places where a run spends most of its frames and tokens. The toolkit applies it by
itself: `T effort` stores it in `state.json → effort`, and `T prep-all` and `T qc --run` read it, so no step passes rates by hand. Two
budgets are the agents' to keep: the densified ranges per reference at analysis time (`analysis-protocol.md` §1) and the single
full-resolution frames per QC pass (`anomaly-catalog.md` §4 step 1). Nothing that a check needs is ever lowered (§2).
