# Own voiceover (ElevenLabs): the voice the user picks, used the way the user picks (contract item 31)

The video model normally makes its own sound: dialogue, action sounds and ambience. With an **own voiceover** the spoken words come from
ElevenLabs instead, in the voice, the language and the accent or dialect the user picked, and the ElevenLabs file itself (the **take**) is the
voice the finished video carries. The user also picks **how** the take meets the picture, the **method** (§2):

- **native**: the take is the generation's **driving audio** (Wan 3.0, Wan 3.0 Prime, Wan 2.7): the model builds the video around it, the lips
  and the action timing follow it, and the generated file's sound is the take. One generation, nothing edited afterwards.
- **lipsync**: any model generates the speaking scene, then Higgsfield's **Sync Lipsync 3** (`sync_so`) re-syncs the mouth to the exact take,
  and its output carries the take. Two generations per voiced video.
- **overlay**: any model generates the video without dialogue (narration, a product review, a story told over the pictures), and the take is
  placed over it: on its own track in the CapCut project, and mixed into the deliverable by `T voiceover mix`.

Where it applies: **Create**, asked at the start before the model menu (`awl-start` step 2b); **Free**, only when the user's requirement asks
for it, never offered, suggested or asked otherwise (`awl-free-plan` step 2b). Never in **Recreate** or **Edit Mode**: their original audio
returns (item 19). The tools are the ElevenLabs MCP (`mcp__elevenlabs__*`, deferred: load them with ToolSearch), `sync_so` (through
`generate_video`, with the ledger like every paid call) and `T voiceover` (gather-only: it measures, records and mixes; it never judges). Read
only the sections a step names.
