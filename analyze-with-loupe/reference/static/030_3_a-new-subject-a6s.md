## 3 A new subject (A6s)

Static keeps the references' **delivery** and changes their **subject** (the transfer rule of `creative-direction.md` §5.0: the mechanism
and the style are carried, the subject is new; the copy test applies: one yes makes the idea a remake).

- **The run's niche, a new subject** (§10). Every subject comes from the niche the user locked at A5, told in the references' craft
  (their hooks, telling, devices, pacing, edit and sound); never a reference's own subject: references about a dark-psychology book get
  another book, author or concept, references about one historical event another event. More videos of the run never repeat or retell
  a subject it made (`T static subjects --run RUN` lists them).
- **No religion and no superstition** (§11): a subject about either, or whose telling needs either, is not in the pool at all (never
  told with it cut out); the pool holds subjects whose story stands with none of it.
- **Factual niches stay factual.** When the references tell real facts (history, science, psychology, biographies, true crime), every
  subject is real and every claim the script makes is sourced: at least two independent sources per subject found with WebSearch and
  WebFetch (at most four searches per subject), written to the video's fact sheet; a claim no source holds is cut, never invented.
- **Characters decide the kind of subject** when the assets hold them (read who they are from their photos and descriptions):
  - a known real person: real, sourced facts (their rise, a turning point, a lesser-known story), told the references' way;
  - an ordinary role (a farmer, a nurse, a soldier): a story, an anecdote or a drama of their world that fits the references' telling;
  - a cartoon or a children's character: a children's story with the references' rhythm, in a register safe for children;
  - several characters: a subject they can share.
- **No characters**: the subject comes from the run's niche and the references' data (what their audience reacts to), a subject the
  references do not cover.
- **Brand guardrails** of the documents apply as in every mode (`brand-guardrails.md` §4).
- **The pool**: the requested count plus three candidates at least (never under five), each scored 1–5 on stop power (the hook it allows),
  curiosity gap, fit to the spec, freshness (not already everywhere in the niche), image-ability (stills can carry it), and for facts
  source strength; ties go to stop power. Each candidate: a title, its hook line, why it fits (one line), and for facts its sources' count.
