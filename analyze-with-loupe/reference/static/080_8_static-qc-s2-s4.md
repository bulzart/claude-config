## 8 Static QC (S2–S4)

The agent views and listens through the facts; `qc_fixes` (ask, auto, off) decides fixes as in every sub-mode (contract item 27).
- **Narration**: `narration-words` lists the lines to check (the heard share of their words under the threshold); the duration within the
  spec's range; the loudness; one speaker throughout.
- **Every image, viewed** (Read the file): it shows what its line says; the character matches their photo or anchor (face, hair, build,
  clothing, age); hands, faces, bodies and counts are right (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.13 for illustrated, painted and cartoon pictures, §2.17 for identity); no stray
  text, letters, logos or watermarks; the style lock holds across the set; the caption band is free of faces and key detail. A failure is
  a regenerate reason in API mode (by `qc_fixes`); in prompts mode it is listed for the user, never regenerated by the API.
- **The edit**: the preview watched at every image's start and at three caption times; each image on its line; the captions match the
  words; no black frame or jump; the duration and loudness of `slideshow.json`; the CapCut timeline read back (`capcut_read_timeline`) and
  `T capcut-text-style --check` at 0.
- **The craft**: the hook lands in the first second, the payoff lands, and the copy test still says no (`creative-direction.md` §8).
