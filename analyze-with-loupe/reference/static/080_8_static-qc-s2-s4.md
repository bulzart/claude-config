## 8 Static QC (S2–S4)

The agent views and listens through the facts; `qc_fixes` (ask, auto, off) decides fixes as in every sub-mode (contract item 27).
- **Narration**: `narration-words` lists the lines to check (the heard share of their words under the threshold); the duration within the
  spec's range; the loudness; one speaker throughout; each strong line's `pitch_st` and `timbre_sim` against the cloned lines' spread (§6).
- **The bed** (`T music measure`, §6): no word heard in it alone; the narration's words heard the same with it under it at the project's
  gain; its level over the lines rising and falling with the story's beats, not against them; still heard in the pauses.
- **Every image, viewed** (Read the file): it shows what its line says; the character matches their photo or anchor (face, hair, build,
  clothing, age); hands, faces, bodies and counts are right (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.13 for illustrated, painted and cartoon pictures, §2.17 for identity); its size and aspect the format's (`off_aspect` for the user's
  images); no stray text, letters, logos or watermarks; the style lock holds across the set; the caption band is free of faces and key detail;
  one picture, not panels with a seam; the sizes of people, animals and things against each other as in life (§5). A failure is
  a regenerate reason in API and Klein mode (by `qc_fixes`); in prompts mode it is listed for the user, never regenerated by the API.
- **An anchor, before it is used** (it is copied into every image that shows its character): the look of `characters.md`, and no ornament
  that reads as a letter or symbol (§5).
- **The edit**: the preview watched at every image's start and at three caption times; each image on its line; the captions match the
  words; no black frame or jump; the duration, the loudness and the frames (`video_frames` = `expected_frames`) of `slideshow.json`; the
  moves as planned (`T edit-profile deliverables/video-NN-clean.mp4 --timeline 40-briefs/video-NN-timeline.json`: each image's zoom and
  pan against its `moves`, and a jitter at the references' level); each effect at its moment (frames around it with `T frames`): a keyed
  overlay leaves no green and a transition hides its cut, a blended one leaves no dark box, a sound lands on its moment and sits under
  the voice, the `refused` list of `capcut-effects` empty; the
  CapCut lint without `media-unlinked`; the CapCut timeline read back (`capcut_read_timeline`) and
  the texts' look against the plan's `captions` (font, size, colours, position) as `capcut_read_timeline` gives them; the house text
  check (`capcut-text-style`) is not used in Static.
- **No religion and no superstition** (§11): every image viewed for any sign of either (a cross on a spire or a throne, a halo, an altar,
  a figure that reads as clergy or as an idol, a symbol in a pattern) and every script line and caption read for them; a hit is a
  regenerate reason by QC fixes (off: named in the brief and the wrap-up).
- **One channel** (§10): the video's subject is of the run's niche, its images in the run's style lock, its captions in the run's text
  look, its voice the run's narrator.
- **The thumbnail and the title** (§12): the thumbnail viewed at full size and at feed size (its `-small.png`): the subject and its
  feeling read at a glance, its words (if any) letter for letter, the run's style, nothing of §11 or item 6; the title promises what
  the narration keeps.
- **The craft**: the hook lands in the first second, the payoff lands, and the copy test still says no (`creative-direction.md` §8).
