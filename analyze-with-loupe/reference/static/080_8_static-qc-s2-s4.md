## 8 Static QC (S2–S4)

The agent views and listens through the facts; `qc_fixes` (ask, auto, off) decides fixes as in every sub-mode (contract item 27).
Every check also reads the forecast and the decisions written for the video (§13, `40-briefs/video-NN-brief.md` and
`video-NN-forecast.json`): a measured value is judged against what was chosen and forecast for it, and a miss beyond its tolerance is traced
to its cause before it is accepted. The agent is skeptical of its own result (§13) and may have fresh reviewers look at it cold.
- **Narration**: `narration-words` lists the lines to check (the heard share of their words under the threshold; a heard word counts in
  the line that holds its middle). The captions take ElevenLabs' own word timing (its answer's `timing: elevenlabs`); the transcriber
  starts words about a quarter second early and can miss quiet ones (video 03, 2026-11-09: "Day one. By ten, ..." spoken and timed by
  ElevenLabs, never heard by it), so a listed line is checked against the audio and ElevenLabs' timing before it is called wrong. A
  passage in `tts say`'s `resent_after_a_drop` (this PC's web shield resets connections) may be billed twice: its credits before and
  after against `characters_now` say, and the brief names it; no tag spoken aloud
  (a bracket's words heard as speech) and no sound effect where a delivery tag was meant; the duration within the spec's range; the
  pace on the plan's point within its tolerance and inside the bands (`tts say`'s and `narration-words`' `pace.on_target` and
  `pace.within`; §6 "The pace") and the pause pattern against the forecast; the loudness against the references' (the sidecar's `level`: as delivered inside their range, else one gain toward their median
  within the true-peak ceiling, §6); a speed-up only on the user's yes after delivery, capped, with its `--why` and `--user-ok` (§6, §13); one
  speaker throughout; each passage's timbre against the others (`voice_match.passages_below`, §6); a partial narration (`partial`)
  named with where it stops. A fix is a passage made again through the request (§6), never an edit of the delivered audio.
- **The video's language and approach** (the user, 2026-11-11; §4, §6, §10, §12): every line's English `gloss` is there when the
  language is not English (`lines_without_gloss` of the forecast empty); the script, the title, the thumbnail's words, the captions and
  the labels are in the video's language and the same words as the narration says; the figures, dates and names are said as that
  language says them; `narration-words` compares numbers only in English (`numbers.compared` false elsewhere: the agent reads them);
  the captions' font covers every character (`font_missing_glyphs` empty, else another font of the same look, noted) and, for a
  language the word box cannot place (written without spaces or right to left), the plain caption look is used and named in the brief
  and the wrap-up; the pronunciation is "not checked: listen" in the wrap-up, with the language named, whenever the language is outside
  the narration model's own list (`official` false) and for a first passage in a new language; the video's approach is the one
  recorded and differs from the last video's as §10 says, or the reason is written.
- **The bed** (`T music measure`, §6): no word heard in it alone; the narration's words heard the same with it under it at the project's
  gain; its level over the lines rising and falling with the story's beats, not against them; still heard in the pauses.
- **Every image, viewed** (Read the file): it shows what its line says; the character matches their photo or anchor (face, hair, build,
  clothing, age); hands, faces, bodies and counts are right (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.13 for illustrated, painted and cartoon pictures, §2.17 for identity); its size and aspect the format's (`off_aspect` for the user's
  images); no stray text, letters, logos or watermarks; the style lock holds across the set; the caption band is free of faces and key detail;
  one picture, not panels with a seam; it does what the plan wanted of it (§5, §4b); the sizes of people, animals and things against each other as in life (§5); no subtitle-like
  line (`T image text --run RUN --video NN` lists every line of letters it reads, with its box and whether it sits in the bottom third:
  a long line across the bottom or over the picture that no sign, page or screen carries is a made-up subtitle, a redraw reason, its
  prompt checked for caption words first, §5; letters cut off by the bottom edge, only their tops showing, are one too and the check
  cannot read them, so the bottom edge of every still is looked at: 2026-11-07). A failure is
  a regenerate reason in API and Klein mode (by `qc_fixes`); in prompts mode it is listed for the user, never regenerated by the API.
- **An anchor, before it is used** (it is copied into every image that shows its character): the look of `characters.md`, and no ornament
  that reads as a letter or symbol (§5); a known person's anchor read against their photos (face shape, hair, glasses, build): it reads
  as them, in the run's style.
- **Real things** (§5): every logo the plan places is the official file on its surface, legible at the export size and outside the
  caption band, the patch under it not showing; another brand's mark the model drew stays as drawn (never covered, never a flag: the user, 2026-11-13); a known person
  keeps their anchor's likeness in every still.
- **Drawings** (§5 "Drawings"; the agent's own look at the previews of `T draw render`, at the grid's coordinates, before the edit): the
  drawing sits on what it explains (each leader ends on the thing it names, each highlight covers that region and little else); it starts
  when its word is said (`T draw words`) and each element arrives in the order the narration names things; it keeps off the captions'
  band (`in_caption_band`) and does not hide the subject (`fill_share`); its words are letter for letter the plan's, legible at the
  export size, in the video's language and a font with every glyph (`text_missing_glyphs`, `text_refused`); its colours and line look
  are the references' pattern or the run's style lock (§10); it ends inside its image's span (`past_the_image`, `ends_after_drawing`);
  the image under it does not move (`on_moving_image`); nothing of §11/§11b, no sticker, icon, emoji or decorative arrow, no
  subtitle-like text. A drawing that fails is corrected in the plan and rendered again: local work, free, whatever the QC fixes say.
- **The edit**, read back from the CapCut project (no local render, §7): `capcut_read_timeline`: each image at its line's start for its
  span, contiguous from 0 to the timeline's duration (no gap, no overlap, so no black frame), the narration and the bed on their tracks at
  their offsets and gains; the labels (§5): each on the track "labels" over its image's span, its words letter for letter, beside the
  place it names (the picture viewed with the label positions), no labelled image moving; `T capcut-motion`: every image keyed with
  the timeline's `moves` (the moves themselves are the plan's, from the
  references' measured pattern, and `edges_show` was empty); `T capcut-transitions`: every join the timeline's; each effect on its track
  at its moment (each drawing on its track `draw1`, `draw2` … at its start, covering the canvas), the `refused` list of `capcut-effects`
  empty; the captions: as many as `captions.srt` and the same words as the
  narration, the texts' look against the plan's `captions` (font, size, colours, outline, position, intro) as `capcut_read_timeline`
  gives them; the CapCut lint without `media-unlinked`. What only playing shows (a keyed overlay's leftover green, a transition's look)
  is named in the brief for the user's look in CapCut. The house text check (`capcut-text-style`) is not used in Static.
- **No religion and no superstition** (§11): every image viewed for any sign of either (a cross on a spire or a throne, a halo, an altar,
  a figure that reads as clergy or as an idol, a symbol in a pattern) and every script line and caption read for them; a hit is a
  regenerate reason by QC fixes (off: named in the brief and the wrap-up).
- **Nothing indecent, gory or distressing** (§11b): every image and the thumbnail viewed for skin where clothes belong (a bare chest or
  legs, underwear, only a towel), a shower, bath or bed scene, blood, a wound, a corpse, a face in agony, terror or tears, a person
  lying limp or with closed eyes on a floor, a stretcher or a sickbed (it reads as dead or unconscious: 2026-11-09, still 97; a
  survival pose passes only when the person reads clearly alive: head up or turned, eyes open, moving), and every script line and
  caption read for graphic detail, swearing or an intimate part of the body named (2026-11-09, video 03 line 180: "at her groin"); a hit is a regenerate reason by QC fixes (off: named in the brief and the
  wrap-up). A person whose prompt does not name the clothes from top to bottom is looked at twice (2026-11-09: a man "under a cool
  shower ... and his T-shirt" came back with nothing below the shirt).
- **One channel** (§10): the video's subject is of the run's niche, its images in the run's style lock, its captions in the run's text
  look, its voice the run's narrator.
- **The thumbnail and the title** (§12): the thumbnail viewed at full size and at feed size (its `-small.png`): the subject and its
  feeling read at a glance, its words (if any) letter for letter, the run's style, nothing of §11 or item 6; the title promises what
  the narration keeps.
- **An extra format** (§2b): its stills and its project get every check above, on their own files; each still also against the
  primary's (the same scene, people and things, only its layout changed for the frame), and its labels, logos, aimed zooms and caption
  band read off its own picture, never carried over.
- **The craft**: the hook lands in the first second, the payoff lands, and the copy test still says no (`creative-direction.md` §8).
