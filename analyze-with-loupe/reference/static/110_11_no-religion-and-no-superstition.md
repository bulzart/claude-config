## 11 No religion and no superstition

The user, 2026-11-07: "on the content, nothing about religious should appear or be spoken about such as: God, shaykh, pope, cross, idol
statue, pagan things, rabbi, budism or any superstitious."

No subject, line, caption, picture or sound of a Static video is about, names, shows or alludes to a religion or a supernatural belief:
- God or gods; prophets, saints, angels and demons; clergy and religious figures of any faith (a shaykh, a pope, a rabbi, a priest, a
  monk, a bishop, an imam); scriptures, prayer, worship and rituals;
- places of worship (a church, a cathedral, a mosque, a synagogue, a temple, a shrine) and what furnishes them (an altar, stained glass,
  altar candles);
- religious symbols (a cross or crucifix, a crescent, a star of David, prayer beads, a halo), idols and idol statues;
- pagan and mythological gods and their rites; Buddhism and every other faith;
- superstition: astrology and horoscopes, omens, charms and amulets, curses, fortune telling, spirits and ghosts presented as real.

How it applies:
- **Subjects** (§3; the user, 2026-11-07: "the idea is that not avoid them if they're part of the content, for example if there's a
  story about a famous rabbi that did this and that, then we should not pick the rabbi story at all but rather focus on something that
  just isn't focused around and doesn't mention any religious and superstitious means"): a subject is picked only when its story is
  told whole with none of these. A story about a religious figure, place, event, text or practice, or one whose telling needs any of
  them (a famous rabbi's deeds, a saint's life, a pope, a crusade or a war over faith, a temple's history, a myth of the gods, a curse
  legend), is not in the pool at all: it is never told with the religion cut out. Pick subjects whose story stands with none of it; one
  where a religious detail is only incidental (where a ceremony took place) is told without it, and neither a line nor a still shows it.
- **Scripts** (§4): no line names or alludes to one. A fact that needs one to be told is cut or told without it (Napoleon crowned
  himself emperor: no cathedral and no pope in the line or the still).
- **Pictures** (§5): each prompt describes a secular scene in positive terms (a palace throne hall with tall windows and banners), and
  never names what must stay out, because a prompt that says "no cross" tends to draw one. A town or a skyline has no church tower or
  dome made to stand out; nobody prays; no figure wears clergy clothes.
- **The bed** (§6): no hymn, choir singing sacred words, chant or church organ in its caption or its sound.
- **QC** (§8): every image is viewed for any of these (a cross on a spire or a throne, a halo, an altar, a figure that reads as clergy
  or as an idol, a symbol in a pattern) and every script line read for them. A hit is a regenerate reason by the run's QC fixes; with QC
  fixes off it is named in the brief and the wrap-up for the user.
- A place's own name that carries a religious word (Saint Helena, San Francisco) is geography and stays as the name; nothing about the
  saint is said or shown.
