### 4b.5 Expressing it to the model (with §7)
- Describe the item as **part of the scene from the first frame**, inside the environment
  description the official examples use: "<canonical description> <anchor>", e.g. "an open <drink>
  stands on the desk to the right of the laptop", "a <car> is parked at the curb behind the bench".
- Avoid edit verbs ("add", "insert", "place a product") and advertising words ("product shot",
  "hero", "showcase", "brand", "advertisement"). They pull models toward entrances and ad framing.
- Say that it persists ("stays on the desk throughout", "stays parked"). Write any interaction as a
  chronological event with the hand or the driver named. Add a duplicate exclusion in the model's
  syntax ("only one <product> in the scene").
- **Pin its spot to a fixed neighbour** (CE-019). *Achieve:* the spot named against something that does
  not move ("at the left end of the table, a hand's width in from its left edge, there from the first
  frame"), the same relation restated for every shot and across every cut, and exclusions for the move
  itself ("no sliding product, nothing that changes place"). *Avoid:* a loosely named spot ("in the centre
  of the table"), which the model re-drew between seconds. *Miss and cause:* V07's can slid along the table
  between 1 s and 2 s; its first request named a loose spot ("in the centre of its length, between the
  roommate and the laptop") and "stays where it is". [OBSERVED once: the corrected request removed the
  slide inside a shot, not the relocation across a cut; not verified.]
- **Name the one hand, not the limbs.** The neighbours that stay are named; the one hand in frame is named with
  the bare empty area ("no other hand is near the <product>"), and no further limb: a sentence that listed a hand
  and a forearm drew a second fist (CE-085, CE-089) and held once (CE-054 a2). Fractions of the frame,
  padded-reference phrases and "no oversized" exclusions are not relied on.
- **Say what is not there.** An empty surface is written as empty ("the desk holds only the laptop; no can
  stands on it at any moment"), and per shot for a multi-shot source ("shot 1: only the laptop and the
  planner; no can anywhere"); "out of frame in shot 1" did not hold (CE-078, VERIFIED). When the source's own
  object is handheld and only enters with a hand, padded standing-product references pull a standing product
  onto the nearest desk from frame 0 (1.66× its size), it is picked up and set back at the end: write the
  empty desk, that the product enters in the hand and leaves below the frame edge with it, and never describe
  it as standing (CE-046, VERIFIED).
- **Keep its brand mark.** The clean-plate line carves out the item's own printed label, logo or
  badge (`text-and-format.md` §1): never a bare "no logos", which blanks the product. Its words go
  into QC `--allowed-text`.
- **Start-frame models:** the 9:16 start frame already contains the item at its anchor, with a
  contact shadow and the scene's light. It is the literal first frame (an end frame the literal last),
  so its product is a photoreal render in the scene, never a pasted asset sample: check it against the
  canonical template, with the paste test (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.15) and `T scale --frame` before animating.
- **Reference models:** the product views are bound to their role and ride padded (the product small in a
  large plain field): a tight packshot that fills its image pulls the size up and is left out. The prompt
  states the anchor and that the item keeps the scene's lighting. No reference places the item, in any
  mode: no copy of the source with the product composited in, no layout, guide or placement image; the spot
  and the size are the prompt's (the anchor pinned to a fixed neighbour above, the size against a named
  neighbour, `anomaly-catalog.md` §2.7), with the product's views and the plan's instructions.
- **The original reference (Recreate and Edit Mode, every model;** [U] 2026-10-28: "just let the original
  video as a reference, i believe prompts,images and instructions suffice"**).** The request's only video
  reference is the reference's own footage, silent and unmarked, of exactly the span, orientation and length
  the plan cut: with no inserts, no flip and no padding the original's silent copy as it is (`<references
  folder>/videos_without_audio/<name>`); with inserts the stripped own content (`own/own-silent.mp4`); with a
  flip or a held-frame padding the prepared copy that had it (`prepared/source.mp4`). It rides in the model's
  most preserving input (§5c.2: the edit source, the motion source or the first video reference) and carries
  the performers, their motion, the camera, the framing, the timing, the scene's look and its light; the
  original audio is restored locally after the generation. Nothing that places the product rides beside it,
  and **a guide image cut from a source frame (a crop of the source, the performer blurred or scrubbed) is
  never built, uploaded or bound** ([U] 2026-10-22: "never upload or use it ever again"). A source longer than
  the model's video-reference limit (its guide §2.2, §2.5) is split into clips on its own cuts (§3b.1). An
  exclusion of the original's own content ("do not inherit … branded items of Video 1") names the original's
  items only, never the product the request places.
- **When a reference video drives the motion** (Recreate only; Create never passes a reference),
  it shows no such item, and the model may treat the item as foreign:
  - say explicitly that the item is part of this scene although the reference has none;
  - keep the reference's actions;
  - for an in-hand anchor, name the hand that holds it while the other hand performs the
    referenced gestures.
- **Multi-clip videos (§3b):** the anchor and the item's state are part of every hand-off state,
  restated word for word in the next clip.
- **State what every vessel holds.** Bind the contents to the sample: "the glass on the table holds
  <product> poured from the open can beside it: a bright orange, sparkling drink with fine rising
  bubbles and ice, as in @ImageN". A glass, cup or bowl whose contents are not stated comes back as
  water, or as whatever the model guesses.
- **Asset photos bring their props.** A character or environment photo that shows a glass, a cup or
  a snack makes the model reproduce it. Plan each such prop as the product's contents when it is of
  the product's category, or limit that image's role in the prompt ("Image 4 is only the man's
  identity: face, hair, build and clothing; not its glass, table or background").
