### 4b.6 The placement marker (spot and size, labelled, in every placement reference)
*Achieve:* every reference that shows the model where the product goes and how big it is carries the
**placement marker**: the client's product at the reckoned spot and size (§4b.7), a thin red outline around it
and a short label beside it, "PLACEMENT + SIZE MARKER ONLY - asset sample, not the final look". This holds for
the prepared source video of Recreate and Edit Mode, a layout or guide image, Create's neutral-texture guide and
any other placement image. The request pairs the marker with sentences that make it a placement and size
reference only and ask for a real object rendered in the scene, and with the removal line for every mark (item 4
below). Where the locked model's guide lists size or place as a model limit and no passing-list anchor fits, the
marker is the size-and-place control from the FIRST request, and always for a near-plane spot the user marked
(CE-070, CE-081). *Avoid:* a placement reference without the label, which the model can read as the product's
final look and copy (a pasted sample, a flat sticker, a soft label); sizing words as the lever (every comparable
wording retry was a re-roll); a spot the source does not have. *Miss and cause:* small placed packs and cans
rendered 1.3–2.8× their size in most first takes whatever the words said; with a separate placement image
beside the source, eight takes landed within 1.25× (`wan-3-0.md` §5 lists them). The user then saw that a
composited sample can be taken for the product's real look, and rejected the blurred source-crop guide image:
"never upload or use it ever again" (2026-10-22, [U]).

**Recreate and Edit Mode: the marker lives in the prepared source.** The product is composited into the silent
prepared copy (`RUN/10-media/<V>/prepared/`) with `T placeholder` at the spot and pixel height of §4b.7, one
`--window` per shot, punch-in or set-up, and that labelled video is the source the request binds as Video 1.
**A guide image cut from a source frame (a crop of the source, the performer blurred or scrubbed) is never
built, uploaded or bound in Recreate or Edit Mode,** and no separate placement image rides beside the prepared
source: the marker in the video carries spot and size.

**Create: the neutral-texture guide.** Create has no video reference. Where a placement image is needed, it is a
neutral texture of the room's colour only, never cut from a reference or source frame (no reference frame is ever
an input in Create), with the product marked by `T placeholder --image`, so it carries the same outline and
label. It is bound as one more image reference, for spot and size only.

**Start and end frames are the exception.** A `start_image` or an `end_image` is reproduced as the literal first
or last frame of the output, so it never carries a label, an outline or any mark (they would come back pixel
for pixel). The product in such a frame is already a photoreal render in the scene, never a pasted asset
sample, and the frame passes the paste test (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.15) and `T scale --frame` before it is
used (`assets-and-substitution.md` §5).

Build the marker locally, no credit:
1. **The product render.** The client's render of the planned variant (RGBA), turned into the scene's
   perspective where it can be (about thirty degrees with a side face, top and base ellipses for the camera
   height, foreshortened when it lies flat: `--aspect-from render` for a render already foreshortened), its
   printed label sharp at the video's pixel scale with a thin edge: a soft label or a thick bevel is copied
   (CE-090, CE-093), and a marker never fixes label legibility (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.9).
2. **`T placeholder`.** `--product-h-cm` and `--product-w-cm` from the registry draw it at its real
   proportions; `--sample` on a neutral light surface near the spot and `--gain` tone it to the room; a soft
   contact shadow, slight blur and grain follow; `--luma-ref` makes it dip with a flash or a fade of the source;
   `--skin` or `--occlusion-mask` keeps a hand or an object that passes in front of it in front. The label goes
   on the side with more free room, or where `--label-side` puts it when that side would cover a face, the key
   action or a hand's path. The `--basis` lines of §4b.7 are recorded in the sidecar with the boxes and the
   drawn size. The tool decides nothing: the spot, the size and the windows are yours.
3. **Look at it.** View the stills it writes (one per window) at full resolution: the product shaded and in the
   scene's perspective, the outline thin and red, the label readable and off the key action, nothing passing
   through the marker.
4. **The request**, in the model's own structure:
   - the marker sentence: "<Video 1 | Image N>: the <product> inside the red outline, and the label beside it,
     are a placement and size marker only, taken from the client's asset sample, not how the <product> looks.
     At exactly that spot and at that size, render a real <product> as an actual object in the scene: <its
     material: a curved aluminium can, a printed paper carton>, lit by the scene's light, with its own shadows
     and a contact shadow on the <surface> it really stands on";
   - the anchor sentence repeats the relation: "<anchor sentence>, exactly where the marker stands in Video 1,
     and it keeps the marker's size"; the relation to the named neighbour is stated once;
   - the removal line, adapted from Wan's official line for auxiliary marks (`wan-3-0.md` §3.6): "The final
     footage completely removes the red outline, the label and every auxiliary mark; no text or watermarks
     appear in the frame", next to the normal clean-plate lines, with the product's own printed label carved
     out ("except the <product>'s own printed label", `text-and-format.md` §1);
   - the neighbours that stay are named; the one hand in frame is named with the bare empty area ("no other
     hand is near the <product>"), and no further limb is named: a sentence that listed a hand and a forearm
     drew a second fist in CE-085 and CE-089 and held once (CE-054 a2). Fractions of the frame,
     padded-reference phrases and "no oversized" exclusions are not relied on.
5. **X4 checks it before a credit is spent:** `T scale --frame` on a still of the marked reference agrees with
   the reckoning of §4b.7; the label is legible and off the key action; the request carries the marker sentence
   and the removal line; no guide image cut from a source frame is in the request; a start frame carries no
   mark.
6. **X9 checks the output:** `T qc` OCR and a full-resolution look at the spot in every window find no label
   text, no outline and no marker residue (the label's words are never in `--allowed-text`); the product passes
   the paste test as a real object, not the sample, and measures at the reckoned size.

**Evidence kept from the old method** (a separate size-and-place guide image, retired on 2026-10-22 for
Recreate and Edit Mode). The old guide was one more image reference beside the source: a crop of the source's
bare surface at the spot, the performer and lettering scrubbed or blurred, with the product composited in. Eight
takes built with it landed within 1.25× (CE-034, CE-048, CE-054, CE-057, CE-064, CE-074, CE-076/CE-081, CE-078);
the guides that failed, failed on place or copy, not on size: a crop with the performer or lettering still in it
brought the replaced person, the jersey's sponsor lettering or the monitor's lettering back and was copied as a
flat sticker (CE-067, CE-069), and a flat packshot paste was copied flat (CE-052, CE-093). In the one case
measured (CE-081) the guide shrank a nearest-plane product by about a fifth, not to the guide's own size: start
from the true ratio and read the first output with `T scale`. Seedance 2.5 `video_edit` copied a guide
literally, pose, size, spot, soft label and bevel (`seedance-2-5.md` §5). The labelled marker keeps what worked
(spot and size shown in the scene's own perspective, toned, with a contact shadow) and drops the source crop.
Whether the locked model removes the outline and the label cleanly is unverified until a run measures it: X9
reads every take for residue.
