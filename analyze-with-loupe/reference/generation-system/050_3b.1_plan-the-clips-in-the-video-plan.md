### 3b.1 Plan the clips (in the video plan, before any request)
1. **Put seams on the reference's own cuts first.** Where the content family cuts anyway, a clip
   boundary on that cut is invisible:
   - each clip holds whole shots, and the join is the planned hard cut (`concat --seams cut`);
   - across a cut only the world must carry over, not the pixels: identities, wardrobe, layout,
     light, props, the placed product's anchor and state (§4b), and the ambience.
2. **Use a continuous seam only where no usable cut exists** (one take longer than the maximum).
   Put it on a calm moment: low motion and no speech, such as between sentences or on a held beat.
   Never mid-action, mid-word, on the hook or on the payoff.
3. **Use as few clips as the length allows**, each within the model's range. Every seam is a risk.
4. **Write a hand-off state for every seam**, as a ledger row (§3) at the seam time:
   - positions and poses, and who holds what in which hand;
   - object and liquid states, and the placed product's anchor;
   - camera position and framing, and the light;
   - the audio state (who speaks next, the ambience).
   The next clip's prompt restates it word for word.
5. **Speech never crosses a seam.** Each clip's lines are complete sentences.
6. **Keep everything else identical:** the same references in the same order, the same parameters
   (resolution, audio flag, mode), and the same wording for identities, wardrobe, setting, light and
   style in every clip.
7. **Recreate: the whole source in one generation when it fits; more only when it must** (the user, 2026-11-05; a
   Free `recreate` video too). The maximum is the locked model's, as Higgsfield answers it live, never a number kept
   here (models change): `models_explore get` (E4 at A6r, again at A8) gives the output's range (its `duration`
   parameter) and any limit its video-reference media lists; only a limit the live answer does not give (often the
   longest video reference) comes from the model's guide §2.2, and the plan says which number came from where
   (`state.json → model_limits`). The maximum is the hard limit: a note that a shorter length works best (Seedance
   2.5's edit source "≤20 s best") never lowers it. A source whose generated length (inserts excluded) fits both (the
   video reference it rides as, and the output) is sent whole, in ONE generation, its hard cuts included: with a 15 s
   maximum a 14 or 15 s video is one generation, never two (never 7.5 + 7.5 s), and with a 30 s maximum
   a 27 s video is one generation. A take that comes back a little longer than its source (16 s for 15 s) is trimmed
   to the source's length (item 19, E5). A source at most 0.4 s over the maximum (15.2 or 15.4 s against 15 s) is one
   generation too: only its first <maximum> seconds are sent, the video ends there and its audio is cut with a short
   fade-out. It is split under the maximum only when that is necessary, and the clip plan says why: the source
   changes place or ambience across a cut (one clip per place: a 27 s video whose surroundings change at 15 s may go
   as 15 + 12 s, when the plan judges that better), or it is a case one generation already failed: a person
   alternating with person-free cutaways or close-ups, or a run of hard cuts the model merged (below). A source
   longer than that takes the fewest generations its length needs (its length divided by the maximum, rounded up),
   split on its own cuts nearest the even parts, or where its places change, as its data shows.
   **Edit Mode** keeps the earlier rule: a cut-heavy source goes in separate clips even under the maximum.
   *Avoid:* one clip for a reference that alternates a person with person-free close-ups, or whose many hard cuts
   (ten in 15 s) the model already merged once.
   *Miss and cause:* with a person identity reference the model lost the cuts of the first seconds, rendered
   the cutaways as face shots or as ghost dissolves, dropped the placed prop in a face shot framed differently
   from the opening shot, and lost an emotion beat at the end; the numbered shot list and "only the screen, no
   man" did not hold (CE-079, CE-083, CE-086). *Achieve (planned, not yet verified):* one clip per face run and
   one per cutaway run, cut on the reference's own cuts, each at least the model's minimum duration, joined by
   hard cuts, with the product's place named per shot.
8. **Create (command item 35): takes, not shots.** The clips are the take plan's clean continuous takes, as few as the video needs
   (one when its length fits the locked model's longest duration and one continuous shot can show it; another for a new place, a
   jump in time, an angle the edit cannot make, a layout's panel or a length beyond the maximum; never one generation per shot); the edit's cuts are made in CapCut from them, so the seam
   rules above stand only where one continuous action spans two takes, and a cut-heavy pacing comes from cutting the takes, not
   from more generations (item 7 is for Recreate and Edit Mode sources).
