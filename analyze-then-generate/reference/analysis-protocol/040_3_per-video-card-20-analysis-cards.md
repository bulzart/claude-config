**Create cards are also viral cards.** Besides the fields above, a Create card carries the viral layer of `viral-browser.md` §3.2, so that each reference is read once: the product layer kept apart from the mechanism layer; the ranked drivers of spread and whether it is attention-only or commerce-proven; the one-sentence product-agnostic mechanism, its dependence on its own product and its transplant needs; the audience (who, the awareness stage, the pain or desire) and the mentality (the mental shortcuts it uses, the emotion at the hook, the middle and the payoff, what got them hooked); the engagement mechanics; commerce signals when a metrics file is supplied; and the one-line `Facts:` JSON (its `category` is optional in Create). The product layer is recorded and then discarded for ideation (`viral-browser.md` §14.1).

**The audio context in each mode.** Every card, in Recreate, Create and Viral-Browser, carries the three lines `Audio context:`, `Said:` and `Words and sound together:` (a baseline's light card, the first one), written from `20-analysis/audio-context.md` and the transcript; `T audio-context --check --cards` exits 1 and lists the cards in which one of them is missing or empty (a form check; it writes nothing). **Recreate:** context only, so that the plan knows what is said and which beats the restored original audio must still fit; only the silent copy is ever uploaded. **Create:** the craft of the speech may be borrowed (the hook type, the delivery, the way the viewer is addressed), never the lines, the claims or the subject. **Viral-Browser:** the words are evidence of the hook, the promise, the audience and the mentality (`viral-browser.md` §4.3, §4.5).

**Reading the sound numbers.** `audio-profile.json` and the digest give numbers and no label; the card's sound-design line is your reading of them together with the picture and the transcript. Rules of thumb (guidance, not enforced anywhere): a harmonic ratio outside speech (`harmonic_ratio_non_speech`) of about 0.55 or more with an audible non-speech share (`non_speech_audible_share`) of 0.15 or more, or a beat strength (`beat_strength`) of about 0.35 or more, usually means a music bed; spectral flatness (`spectral_flatness_median`) near 0 is tonal and near 1 noise-like; a steady-tone share (`peak_frequency_steady_share`) near 1 is one steady tone such as a beep, not music; `energy_change_db` (last third minus first) says whether the sound rises or falls; `hits` with `on_cut` say where stings and impacts land. Where the numbers disagree with the picture or the transcript, say so on the card.
