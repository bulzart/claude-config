### 4.9 Niche concentration and the bias plan

The main session reads `corpus-stats` and writes its reading: the category table (which niches the references show, with the figures), whether one niche dominates (rule of thumb: a niche that is at least 40 % of the references' cards, `niche_dominance_threshold`; there can be two), the creator concentration (the `creator_group` table: does one creator carry many of the videos), and the **bias plan**: which of the controls in §5 apply (they always apply; the plan states how strongly, in the form "dominant niche: <niche>, <k> of <n>, so a pick from it carries a written defence against the best outside-niche alternative and the pool should hold at least six other niches"). No script says "dominant" or "none": the main session says it, with the figures, and records any exception and its reason in the bias log (§5 item 11).
