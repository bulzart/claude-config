### 8.3 The gates

The main session applies the six gates when it chooses the picks (§8.4), reading `T candidates table` and the evidence; the numbers are rules of thumb (`rules_of_thumb.gates`). A candidate is **eligible to be a pick** when it passes all six; for each candidate that does not, the main session writes the failed gate and its reason (the "ineligible" list in `ranking.md`). The red team's `fails` reading also removes a candidate. The main session may depart from a number when the evidence says so and writes down which and why; a gate is never lowered silently to fill the table (§8.6). The gate fields (`price_fit`, `compliance`, `excluded`) are recorded by the scoring agent (§8.2), and the contribution comes from `T econ`. `T candidates table` puts everything recorded side by side, one row per candidate: niche, type, origin (hidden with `--blind`), status, the rating of each criterion, the plain weighted sum of the criteria rated (`--exclude-criteria fit` leaves one out, for a what-if), the confidence, the red-team reading, the price-fit and compliance fields, the economics of every market, an evidence cell (rows, grade A or B rows, distinct sites) and the pick order; `--sort` (id, name, niche, type, status, confidence, evidence, pick, weighted, `rating:<criterion>`, `economics:<market>`), `--desc`, `--statuses`, `--out` and `--json` are for reading. It marks no winner and applies no gate.

| gate | rule |
|---|---|
| **G1** price and economics | a retail price (or a variant or bundle) inside the user's band, a contribution at or above `gates.min_contribution_pct` (25 % of net revenue) that **came from `T econ --cand`** (for a Global market, the weakest market named, read side by side in `candidates table`), the calculated grade C economics row, **and** an independent grade A or B economics row for a sourced input behind it |
| **G2** compliance | status `clear` or `resolved` (`open` and `unknown` fail), **and** at least one positive independent grade A or B compliance row; `clear` beside negative compliance rows fails |
| **G3** exclusions | not in the user's exclusions |
| **G4** independent evidence | positive independent grade A or B rows across the demand, commercial and competition lanes from at least `min_independent_evidence` (2) **distinct sources**, one of them in the commercial lane |
| **G5** floors | after the evidence rules of §5 item 6, `demand`, `commercial`, `economics`, `compliance` and `fit` each rated at least 3 (`gates.min_scores`): high scores elsewhere never carry a candidate whose evidence says the opposite |
| **G6** confidence | at least `min_confidence_for_pick` (medium) |
