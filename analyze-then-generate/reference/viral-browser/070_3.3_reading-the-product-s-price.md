### 3.3 Reading the product's price

A price seen, said or implied is recorded as a signal with its currency, not a fact about the market: "the video says it costs less than a coffee" is [E] about the video; the market price is researched (§7). Implied price tier (`impulse`, `mid`, `considered`) is an [I] label the synthesis defines from what the corpus shows; a card sets `price_tier` only when the video shows or says a price (§3.2), and the main session defines the tiers.
