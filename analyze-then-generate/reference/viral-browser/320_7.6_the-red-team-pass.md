### 7.6 The red-team pass

After the ratings (§8.4) the main session reads `T candidates table` with the evidence, applies the evidence rules and the gates as it will for the final choice (§8.3, by its own reading), and names the **red-team targets**: the candidates that would be picks **plus the next two** (`redteam_extra`), so the final picks are the ones attacked, not a pre-gate top five. One red-team agent takes them (the main session passes the ids in the prompt). For each: "assume this product fails within ninety days; what is the most likely cause?" It looks for the strongest evidence of: saturation or price collapse, margin collapse (a cost it missed, a fee, a return rate, and first of all the real supplier price, carton size and weight of the exact specification), a compliance or platform block, a fad already peaking, a defect pattern in reviews, an IP conflict. Findings are registered as evidence rows (`--signal negative`, `--independent yes`) and summarised in `30-research/redteam.md`: per candidate, the attacks tried, the strongest counter-evidence, the residual risk, and a reading (**survives**, **weakened**, **fails**) naming the criteria whose ratings to revisit. The main session records the reading (`T candidates set --id C## --redteam survives|weakened|fails --note "<the strongest counter-evidence ids>" --by main --reason "red team"`), and a candidate that **fails** is out of the picks: it is eliminated with the red team's evidence ids as the reason (`--status eliminated --reason "red team: EV###, EV###"`) when the failure rests on facts that were obtained, and listed as **watch** (`--status watch --reason "<the one thing that would qualify it>"`, §8.6) when the reading is conditional on a fact that could still be obtained (a supplier quote, a tariff ruling, a narrower brief); either way the reading itself stays `fails` in the `redteam` field. After the re-rating and the elimination, the main session reads the table again: **any new entrant to the targets is red-teamed too** (repeat until every target has a reading; at most two rounds). No command refuses a pick that lacks a reading: the main session does not record one.
