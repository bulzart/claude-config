### 13.5 Niche-audit agent (fresh context; `subagent_type: general-purpose`)

```
You audit the niche vocabulary of a /analyze-then-generate Viral-Browser run. <persona block from §1>
Run: <RUN>   Library (ATG): …   Toolkit (T): …
Why: the pool balance, the dominant-niche reading and the two-picks-per-niche limit all COUNT niches, so a niche that sits under two names escapes them. No script compares names or counts words: you are the check.
Read: the section file <ATG>/reference/viral-browser/230_5_*.md (§5 items 3 and 4). Do NOT open the videos, the mechanism map or any dossier, and do not guess where a candidate came from. You MAY read the first six lines of a reference card (title, Facts line, Format and style, Subject, Product layer) to see what product a card label stands for, because a label alone does not say it; no other line of a card.
Inputs: `T niches list --run <RUN>` (each niche, its member labels, how many cards and candidates sit in it), `T niches propose --run <RUN>` (every label in
use, its count and the niche it is recorded under) and `T candidates list --run <RUN> --blind --md` (names, types and niches).
For every pair of niches ask: would a shopper, a supplier search or a marketplace category treat them as the same place to look for the product (the same buyer,
the same use, the same shelf)? Synonyms that share no word count, and so does an activity named in one niche and its objects in the other when the products overlap. Look for the opposite too: a niche so broad that it holds products with different buyers and uses,
and a word that recurs in many names (a material, a size) without being a niche marker.
Write your report to <RUN>/30-research/niche-audit.md (name EVERY niche of `niches list` by its exact name, so the main session can see that you looked at each one): (0) one line per niche, `<niche>: ok | merge into <niche> | too broad: <why>`; (1) pairs you judge to be one niche, with the entries that show it and your confidence (merge: from -> to); (2) pairs that look
alike but are distinct, one line of reason each; (3) niches too broad to count as one (propose a split; do not apply it); (4) words that recur across names and may point at a split, or are only a material or a modifier and not niche markers; or "no merges needed".
Change nothing: run no command that writes (`niches add|map|merge|audit`, `candidates add|set|rate`); do not call Higgsfield or CapCut tools, spawn agents,
use the Workflow tool, ask questions, log in, buy anything or contact anyone.
Final message: one line per recommendation (merge from -> to; distinct pair and reason; too-broad niche; non-marker word).
```

The main session then reads the report and decides, applying what it accepts: `T niches merge --run RUN --from … --to … --reason "<sentence>" --by main` for each merge (the members move with it and the candidates are renamed), `T viral-stats --run RUN` again when a merge touched a card label, and last `T niches audit --run RUN --by "niche-audit agent <id>" --result "<merges applied, pairs kept distinct and why, or none needed>" --report <RUN>/30-research/niche-audit.md`. Pairs kept distinct are written in the result and in the bias log (nothing needs to be recorded to keep two niches apart). `niches audit` records who reviewed which set of niches, what they found and where the report is; it checks nothing about the report. A niche added or merged after the audit was not reviewed by it: the record lists the niches the audit reviewed, and the main session has a fresh agent audit again before it relies on the vocabulary.
