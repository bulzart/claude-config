### 4.6 What hooked them — the essence — `20-analysis/viral-essence.md`

At most twelve lines, product-free:
- **The essence**: three to five sentences on why this set spread, in product-free words: what the viewer sees and feels, how fast, what the price perception is, and which problem or desire it taps.
- **The fit profile**: the **hard requirements** (a product that fails one is out), the **soft preferences** (each adds fit), and the **anti-patterns** (what does not fit this approach), each tied to the mechanism it comes from. The profile holds **mechanism requirements only** (what the product must show, how fast, with or without sound, for which viewer). The brief's constraints (the price band, the landed-cost ceiling, owned inventory, the compliance screen) go on a separate line under it, **"Brief constraints: read under economics, compliance and gates G1 and G2, not under fit"**: a cost failure written into the profile is counted twice (once under `fit` and again under `economics`).
- **What we do not infer**: the claims the corpus cannot support (a size effect, a price effect, a causal claim from winners alone).
