### 3.2 The card

- **The `Facts:` line is JSON on one line, starting at the beginning of the line** (`T viral-stats` tabulates it). **`category` is the niche of the product's main use, written as a short label taken from the material** (whatever the video shows; **no script lists, expects or knows any niche**, and a video that shows no product category says `non-product`). The niche-bias controls (§5) count niches, so one niche must not hide behind several spellings, and **the agents, not the scripts, decide which labels are the same niche**: after the first pass the main session reads the labels in use (`T niches propose --run RUN`: every label on a card or a candidate with its count and the niche it is recorded under, plus `unmapped_labels`), decides which belong together, and records the **niche vocabulary** as run data with `T niches add --name "<niche>" --members "<label>,<label>" --by main --reason "<why>"` and `T niches map --label … --to … --by main --reason "<why>"` (file `20-analysis/niche-vocabulary.json`, which keeps the history). From then on `T viral-stats` and `T candidates add` / `summary` file every label under the niche the agents recorded for it, and as written otherwise; a label no agent has placed is reported (`recorded_in_vocabulary` false in the viral-stats category table, `niche_not_recorded_in_the_vocabulary` when a candidate is added, `unmapped_labels` in `niches list`) and goes back to the main session to place: nothing is refused. A fresh agent reviews the vocabulary before the shortlist (§5 item 3, §13.5). Every reference card carries a `category`: `viral-stats` tabulates the cards that have one and lists `cards_without_facts` and `cards_with_unreadable_facts`, and a missing label understates the niche shares the main session reads, so it is fixed (or noted in the bias log) before the table is read. Every other label (`format`, `hook_type`, `demo_type`, `creator_style`, the mechanisms) is a **short label of two to six words** invented from the material, never a sentence: the long description belongs in the card's body (a label that is a sentence is unique on every card, so `viral-stats` can only list it and the main session has to reconcile each one by hand). **One meaning gets one label**: the main session merges synonyms in `20-analysis/labels.md` and rewrites the Facts lines. `product_form` is the physical or digital form in a few words (the details stay in the card's Product layer line). **`price_tier` is set only when the video shows or says a price** (a tag, a spoken price, a number on screen); otherwise the key is left out, and the synthesis defines the tiers (§3.3). **`voice_type`** is the card agent's own reading of what is heard (`on-camera`, `voice-over`, `sung`, `rap`, `chant`, `text-to-speech`, `none`, `mixed`): the transcriber cannot tell speech from lyrics and the digest labels nothing, so the card agent decides from the transcript, the sound numbers and the picture. **`shots_seen`** is the number of shots the card agent counts on the storyboard, kept beside the measured cut count of `video.md` (the shot detector is a measurement with limits). **`creator_group`** is an anonymous id in the form `g01`, `g02`… shared by the videos of one creator: the card agents do not write it; **the main session assigns it after the cards are in**, from the handles the cards carry in their headers (the file names) and the metrics file, and writes it into the Facts lines, so that creator concentration shows in the tables without handles. Leave a key out when the video does not show it.
- **Drivers of spread**, ranked, from this set (add others when the material shows them): the **craft** (hook and format), the **product** (novelty, need, wow), the **creator or account** (an existing audience), the **trend or sound** it rides, **paid amplification**, **controversy or engagement bait** ("is this real?"), **timing** (a season, an event) and luck. A driver that is not the product cannot be credited to a product later; when the reach came from the product being a known brand, the mechanism does not transfer to an unknown one.
- **Attention versus commerce.** Entertainment can spread without anyone buying. `commerce_signal` is `strong` only with evidence of buying intent (a shop tag, a cart, "where do I buy" comments, a price and a link, a sold counter); `attention_only: true` marks a video that spread without such evidence. Attention-only videos still teach mechanisms; they never count as proof that a product sells.
- **Audio context (every mode).** The three lines are mandatory in every card and come from `20-analysis/audio-context.md`, the video's transcript and the picture.
  - *What the digest holds:* numbers and facts about the run, never a label. Per video: the transcription `status` (a fact about the run: ok = the transcriber returned words, no-speech = it returned none, no-audio, failed, missing, skipped), the language with its probability, the number of words and the words per minute, the first word and the first word of probability 0.5 or more, the words below probability 0.5, the first sentence, the repeated three-word phrases, the second pass for a video in which no words were found (the same audio transcribed without the voice filter, `T audio-context --run RUN --second-pass`, kept in the digest between runs; words it finds over pure music or a steady tone may be invented), and the sound numbers (loudness, tempo and beat strength, the harmonic share outside speech, spectral flatness near 0 for a tone and near 1 for noise, the strongest frequency and how steady it is, the non-speech share, the energy change).
  - *What the card agent does with it:* it reads those numbers against the picture and the audio and states its own reading: who speaks and the voice type (a low language probability, many words below 0.5, a first word at a very low probability, a repeated phrase or a steady tone that came back as words are signs the agent weighs, not labels it is given), whether the transcript is doubtful and why, and what the sound is. Speech that is not English is translated, with the hook line kept in its own language beside the translation. A song or a trending sound is named only when the lyrics, the tempo or a known format identify it, marked [I]; a line is marked [I] when the numbers make the transcript doubtful and the picture or the lips disagree. `no speech` is written for a video in which no words were found and the picture agrees (a silent-friendly mechanism is a finding); `transcript unavailable: <why>` only for a status of failed, missing or skipped after `T prep-all --run RUN --only <V> --redo --whisper-model medium` has failed.
  - *Limits:* a spoken claim is a claim, not proof, and the speech of a reference is never credited with what the creator, a trend or paid reach carried. In **Create** the same lines are read for the craft of the speech (§14.1); in **Recreate** they are context only (the reference sent to a generation stays silent).
- **Numbers.** When a metrics file exists, its figures go into `20-analysis/metrics.md` (one row per video: views, likes, comments, shares, saves, followers, post date, link, source) and the card cites them; ratios (shares per view, saves per view, comments per view) are computed, not eyeballed. A counter visible on screen is [E] with its frame time. Without numbers, write "not provided"; never estimate views from the look of the video.
- **Handles stay in the working files.** A card is a working file: its header carries the video's file name, which is often a creator's handle, because the main session needs it to assign `creator_group`. A deliverable never carries one (§0 item 6): the report, the product files and the create-handoff cite the group id and the kind of creator, not the handle, except as a cited source.
