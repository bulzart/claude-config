## 12. Easy-to-miss traps

Each trap is owned by the section named at its end; this list only reminds. Read each as a condition: when the situation arises, the rule applies.

- **The references are winners only.** What they share, losers may share; a large account can make anything spread. Use the baseline contrast when it exists, look at creator concentration and at whether the same mechanism appears across unrelated accounts, and grade every hypothesis (§4.7).
- **Attention is not demand.** Satisfying and funny videos spread without sales: weight the commercial lane, and say which references were attention-only (§3.2).
- **Brand dependence.** When a video's reach came from the product being a known brand, the mechanism does not transfer to an unknown one (§3.2, drivers).
- **Chasing the peak.** "Next" means before the crowd: the slope matters more than the level, and a product already in every feed has little room left (§7.3, demand).
- **Region.** US search results do not prove EU demand; EU prices include VAT; units, plug types, voltage, sizes, languages and labelling differ (§2).
- **Timing.** A product found in October for a December window may not arrive; factory holidays stop supply; a gift item sells in a window and then stops. The cultural and religious calendars of the lead countries move demand windows and acceptable creative (§2, §7.3).
- **Look-alikes and IP.** A copy of a branded or patented design is a legal and a marketplace risk: check names, shapes and designs (§7.3, compliance).
- **Reviews tell the failure modes.** Read the 1–3 star reviews of comparable products: durability, "not as shown", sizing, safety (§7.3, voice).
- **Money outside the unit economics.** The first batch is sized to what can be lost and compared with the capital limit; the sales channel decides the fee schedule and the restricted lists; the importer's country decides who pays import VAT and duty and whether a representative is needed; a returns assumption near zero overstates the margin where a statutory withdrawal right applies (§2, §7.0). Some categories raise payment-processor or ad-account restrictions even when legal: note them (§7.3, compliance).
- **Claims.** Health, income, safety and "guaranteed" claims are where products and accounts get stopped: the blueprint stays inside what is provable (§0 rule 7).
- **Sound licensing.** Business and ad accounts need commercially licensed sounds; a trending sound may not be usable (§4.8, §9 item 7).
- **Producibility.** A product that is hard to film honestly is hard to sell with video, and an AI-generated demonstration must not misstate what the product does (§9 item 10).
- **Confirmation.** Looking for reasons a favourite candidate works is the commonest way to be wrong; the red team and the independent-evidence rules exist for this (§5, §7.6).
- **One niche under two names.** A split vocabulary lets a niche slip past the pool balance and the two-per-niche limit; the safeguard is the fresh niche-audit agent, not a script (§5 item 3).
- **A table is a snapshot.** `viral-stats` and every other table show the ledgers as they were when the command ran. Run them again after any change to a vocabulary, a label or a stored result, and take a figure from the stored result, never from an older dossier (§4.1, §8.2).
- **Two rating scales.** Two scoring agents that rate different batches are two scales: one fresh agent revisits the near-misses of both batches after the red team (§8.2).
- **A wave and its tools.** Background agents die with an interrupt of the main turn and stall when the machine sleeps; the toolkit and the shape of the ledgers are not changed while a wave runs; a field added later is named in the scoring brief with the rows that predate it (§7.1, §7.4, §13.2).
- **A damaged ledger.** `candidates.json`, `evidence.json`, `niche-vocabulary.json`, `specs.json` and `photos.json` are read strictly: a file that cannot be parsed stops the command with an error and is never overwritten as if it were empty. Repair the JSON before going on.
- **`--blind` hides the origin, not the ratings.** An agent that must not be steered by status, ratings, confidence, the red-team reading or stored economics (a reference-pack agent) reads `candidates show --product-only` (§13.7).
