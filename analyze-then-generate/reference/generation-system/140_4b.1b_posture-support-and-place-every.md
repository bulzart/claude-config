### 4b.1b Posture, support and place (every anchor)
Cases CE-006, CE-007, CE-012 and CE-013 (2026-10) taught one thing: the prompt must say what the product rests on and why, because a model that is given no real support invents one. The anchor row of the ledger carries four facts, derived from the product's details and the scene, and the prompt and QC quote them (`anomaly-catalog.md` §2.16):
- **Posture its shape allows**, from the registry's dimensions and the affordance sheet: a thin flat package lies flat, label up; a small flat carton lies flat on a bare surface, the way people leave it, and stands on its base only for a reason the plan states (a frontal label that must read, a use beat; CE-059, CE-060); a can stands on its base. A pack whose natural posture is standing that the plan lays flat needs a reference that shows it lying flat in the scene's perspective (the client's render rotated and foreshortened for the camera height): upright references plus "lies flat" rendered it standing, and a top-view reference taught a footprint taller than the strip allowed (CE-037, CE-041).
- **A support that is in the frame:** a fixed bare surface or a large solid object the source frame shows. Not a prop the model has to invent, not one that moves or is carried away, and not paper, cloth or any thin sheet that hands move or write on (CE-021).
- **A place where a person would leave it:** out of the way of a working hand or arm, at the table's near or far edge, not touching the person; the ledger row gives the story reason in one clause.
- **Not the source's beat:** a placed product does not take over a pick-up, use or hand-off that belongs to another object; the prompt names the object that is picked up and says the product stays.
- **The anchor class the locked model renders at size.** *Achieve:* an anchor from the guide's passing
  list, or a size-and-place guide image (§4b.6) where none fits. *Avoid:* sizing words as the control. When
  the model guide lists size as a model limit (`wan3_0_prime`: `wan-3-0.md` §5), the passing list is: in
  the hand that holds the product; swapped for the source's own handheld object; or, only when the plan's
  package is the flat sachet (command A7), lying flat on bare surface. A spot beside a large rigid object of
  the source in the same plane, or in the near plane, is not on the list: it holds only where §4b.1c holds
  and always with a guide (§4b.6). "Form" in a retry is the integration form (§4b.0), never the package
  form: a retry does not put a smaller package in for the planned one (command A7). The guide holds the list
  and its evidence. Sizing words do not carry the plan.
